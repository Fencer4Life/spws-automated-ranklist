## Context

ADR-038 admits an international row only when its source prints POL, before any matching; ADR-105 kept that gate on every path. Three findings of 2 October 2026 show the gate decides on the wrong fact.

**The gate depends on a column some organisers do not print.** The Budapest Cup 2025 prints a Club column where its other brackets print Country (V1 men's épée, V4 women's foil, V4 men's sabre). BOBUSIA Jarosław, 16 SPWS results, placed 5th of 33 and was dismissed: the parser read his club as his country. Fixing the parser leaves the country blank, and a blank country is dismissed too (fail-closed). On 2 October a per-event override file was built to name him, one fencer at a time.

**Our ranklist has foreign fencers, and the gate keeps their international results out.** They fence SPWS events and are stored with the default nationality PL: LYNCH Pat, MURRAY Claire, MCGINNITY Marie (IRL at EVF), WHITLEY Gary (USA, 8 SPWS results), GOLD Oleg and GERTSMAN Alex (ISR), KUZMICHOVA Svitlana and PRAHA-TSAREHRADSKA Nadiia (UKR), LEAHEY John (GBR), KORONA Radosław (NED), ALCSER Norbert (HUN), KULKA Dawid (5 SPWS results, IRL at EVF). Their EVF results are dismissed because they enter for their own federation.

**Nationality is not tracked.** `tbl_fencer.txt_nationality` holds one value, `NOT NULL DEFAULT 'PL'`; on LOCAL 347 fencers are PL, 17 POL and one each CRC, BLR and LTU. Every pipeline writes the printed country or PL (`python/pipeline/stages.py:578`, `python/pipeline/plugins/resolve_fencers.py:344`). The Admin UI only displays it. It cannot say that KULKA fences PPW for a Polish club and entered EVF Budapest, Madrid and Liège 2025/26 for Ireland, and a fencer may represent a different federation from one season to the next.

**Four paths apply four versions of the rule** (code at `439102ff`). `stages._keep_pol_rows` (`python/pipeline/stages.py:89`) gates `s6_resolve_identity` (`:878`) and `ResolveFencers` (`python/pipeline/plugins/resolve_fencers.py:81`). `resolve_tournament_results` has its own copy (`python/matcher/pipeline.py:207`), used by the Admin per-tournament scrape and the legacy orchestrator. The daily EVF sync matches EVF's rows against the roster with any fuzzy score of 85 or more, with no birth-year and no SPWS check (`python/scrapers/evf_sync.py:805`, `:826`).

**Measured on LOCAL** (the international events that stage from an organiser source, every row staged with the country gate off, drafts discarded): the gate stores 361 Polish identity matches. The rule below stores 485: 130 more rows, belonging to 24 roster fencers who have an SPWS start (21 of them exactly one). 16 rows go to PENDING, and 2 identity matches are rejected (KOLLAR Gabriel, SVK, who has no SPWS start).

## Decision

### 1 · An international row is admitted in three steps

For an international event (tournament type PEW, MEW, MSW or PSW, the IMEW and IMSW codes included), each row of the source is decided once, in this order:

1. **Identity: SURNAME, first name and age category** (the user, 2 October). Exactly one roster fencer has the source's surname and first given name, and a birth year that fits the bracket's category. Polish letters are folded and a second given name in the source is dropped. A compound surname counts as one surname. The year before the season's end year is accepted, because EVF categories go by calendar year and an autumn event falls in it. **And** that fencer has at least one stored result in a tournament typed PPW or MPW, in any season (the GP tournaments of 2023/24 are typed PPW). The row is stored and linked. The country the source prints does not matter.

   Identity is an exact comparison, not a fuzzy score. A one-letter typo ("KOWALSKI Pjotr") or another first name ("GOLA Marcin" against GOLA Maciej, which the matcher scores 95) is not the same person. An unknown birth year cannot confirm the category. Two roster fencers who both fit are not an identity. Name aliases are not used.
2. **Fallback.** Otherwise, if the source prints POL, the row is PENDING. It carries a candidate when there is one: the identity fencer without an SPWS start, or else the matcher's best guess. It is never stored automatically. **A PENDING row blocks the event's sign-off** (decision P A). It is written to the draft with no match method. The Phase 5 runner refuses to commit while one remains and lists it in the staging summary. A person resolves it in the event's override file `doc/overrides/<event>.yaml`: an identity entry (scraped name → `id_fencer`) or a `match_method` entry EXCLUDED, then re-stages. The entry names the fencer by surname, first name and birth year (`fencer:`), resolved against the roster of the environment the run reads, because fencer ids differ between LOCAL, CERT and PROD. An identity entry also links a rejected row whose first name is printed in another form (LYNCH Pat printed "LYNCH Patrick"; decision Q1 A of 2 October: one entry per event, no rule change), and at sign-off it confirms the pair, which is written as the fencer's alias. The Admin scrape, the legacy orchestrator and the EVF sync report a PENDING row and store nothing for it.
3. **Rejected.** Everything else is dismissed and listed in the staging summary. It never becomes PENDING, and no fencer is created (ADR-020).

Domestic events are unchanged. Past seasons stay ungated: the ranking entry gate of ADR-103 §6 stays on 2026/27 only, and SPWS participation is enforced at intake.

### 2 · One admission module, called by every path

`python/pipeline/international_admission.py` decides a row (`admit`) and returns the decision (stored, PENDING or rejected), the fencer, the confidence, the folded federation and the reason. It takes the scraped name and printed country, the bracket's category, the season's end year, the roster, and the set of fencers with an SPWS start. Each run reads that set once from the target environment through `fn_spws_starter_ids()`. `s6_resolve_identity` and `ResolveFencers` call it through one shared helper, `stages._admit_international_rows`. `resolve_tournament_results` calls it for the Admin scrape and the legacy orchestrator, and the EVF sync calls it too. Each path keeps its own plumbing. A call for an international tournament without the starter set is an error, never a fallback to the country. `stages._keep_pol_rows` is removed, and the EVF sync's fuzzy-85 match is replaced.

### 3 · Nationality is recorded per season, and fixed for the season

A fencer may represent different federations in different seasons, but **not within a season**. The first start recorded with a nationality fixes it until the end of that season; he may change it from the next season.

- **The fact per season.** `tbl_fencer_nationality` holds one row per fencer and season: `id_fencer`, `id_season`, `txt_country` (a three-letter federation code), the result that fixed it, and its source (a result, or an admin entry). The key is `(id_fencer, id_season)`, so a season has exactly one nationality and different seasons may differ.
- **The evidence per result.** `tbl_result.txt_entered_for` (and `tbl_result_draft.txt_entered_for`) keeps the federation the source printed for that result, or NULL. The value is folded to three letters (PL → POL), and an empty cell or anything that is not three letters is NULL. A CHECK admits three capital letters only. It is written on every stored international result. The commit, the ingest function and a recompute carry it, and the CERT → PROD promotion copies it.
- **What fixes it.** The earliest-dated stored result of the season that carries `txt_entered_for`; a tie goes to the lower `id_result`. The ingestion order does not matter, so a re-ingest gives the same answer. A start in the same season that prints another federation does not change it. It is listed in `vw_fencer_nationality_conflict` for review.
- **Who writes it.** An AFTER trigger on `tbl_result` (`trg_result_season_nationality`) recomputes the fencer's season whenever a result with a federation is stored, moved or deleted. Every path is covered: the draft commit, the event replace, the ingest function, the seed load. With no evidence left, the season row goes. An admin entry (`enum_source` ADMIN) is kept as entered; until O2 is built, only `service_role` writes one. Authenticated users read the table and the view; anon reads neither.
- **What it is not.** Domestic pages print no federation, so a season with no international start has no nationality recorded. The season nationality is not read by the admission (§1), the scoring or the ranking, and a change to it enqueues no recompute.
- `tbl_fencer.txt_nationality` stays as it is for now: Stage 0's dedup reads it (ADR-056). Its retirement is open (below).

## Alternatives considered

1. **Keep the country gate and name the exceptions in a per-event override file** (built on 2 October as a `nationality` section of `doc/overrides/<event>.yaml`, in the unpushed commit `6630fd67`). Rejected: the rule would then depend on what each organiser prints, every page without a country needs a hand-written file, and the foreign fencers of our ranklist stay out although they fence SPWS events. Decision O1 A removed the section, its Budapest file and P3.OV16–18. The Engarde parser's heading fix (ENG.NAT.01–02) stays.
2. **Identity alone, against any roster entry.** Rejected: 56 roster fencers on LOCAL have no SPWS start. A foreign fencer enters our ranklist only through SPWS participation (KOLLAR Gabriel's two results).
3. **Enforce SPWS participation at ranking time, for every season**, by switching the entry gate on for 2024/25 and 2025/26 too. Rejected by the user (decision B): it removes 9 and 20 fencers from the rankings of those closed seasons. The intake enforces it instead.
4. **ADR-038's rejected alternative 1, keep an AUTO_MATCHED row whatever its country**, was rejected because a name collision across nationalities would link a foreign fencer to a Pole. It is answered here: an identity match now needs the exact surname, the exact first given name and a birth year that fits the category, two namesakes who both fit are not an identity, and the fencer must have an SPWS start. The 130 newly admitted rows measured on LOCAL all belong to 24 roster fencers with SPWS results. What remains possible is a foreign namesake, with a fitting birth year, of one of our SPWS starters; none appeared in the 18 events. ADR-038's other concern, foreign rows flooding the identity queue, cannot recur: only a row printed POL can become PENDING.
5. **Nationality as validity periods** (a federation from one season until it changes). Rejected: one value per season under the season lock is simpler, and correcting one season would split a period.
6. **Nationality derived from the results alone**, a view over the printed federations. Rejected as the record: there is no stored fact to review or correct. The per-result column is kept as the evidence.
7. **Several nationalities per season with one marked chosen** (the first sketch, 2 October). Superseded by the season lock: a season has exactly one.
8. **Identity as the matcher's AUTO_MATCHED** (a fuzzy score of 95 or more with a fitting birth year; the first draft of this ADR). Rejected by the user ("SURNAME, Name, Age category"): the matcher scores "GOLA Marcin" 95 against GOLA Maciej, and a typo of the first name 95 too. The matcher still supplies the PENDING candidate.

## Consequences

- **Code.** The new admission module and its tests; the paths call it; `stages._keep_pol_rows` and the EVF sync's own match go. Per bracket, the staging summary lists the source N, the highest place, the rows stored, how many of them were not printed POL, the PENDING rows and the rejected rows. Below the table it lists every PENDING row with its candidate and the override file that resolves it. Sign-off refuses any draft row without a match method, with or without a fencer. INTL.POL.01–06, the matcher's 4.15–4.18 and 4.61–4.68, and P3.S6.7 are amended: the country no longer gates, and a foreign namesake without an SPWS start is still rejected.
- **Schema.** Migration `20261003000001_identity_intake_season_nationality.sql` adds `tbl_result.txt_entered_for`, `tbl_result_draft.txt_entered_for`, `tbl_fencer_nationality`, its trigger and recompute function, `vw_fencer_nationality_conflict` and `fn_spws_starter_ids()`. It re-creates `fn_commit_event_draft` and `fn_ingest_tournament_results` to carry the column. Grants follow ADR-083 (deny by default). The seed export is schema-driven for `tbl_result`, so it carries the column. `tbl_fencer_nationality` is not exported: the trigger rebuilds it when the seed loads (ADR-036).
- **Data (2 October).** Of the 18 international events of 2024/25 and 2025/26 on the list, 14 stage from an organiser source. Munich 2025 and Chania have only the EVF archive page, Salzburg's d'Artagnan event page is not expanded into brackets, and Munich 2024 is refused for its combined brackets and a duplicated code (ADR-105). Under the exact rule the 14 hold 424 results against 347 stored, with 18 PENDING rows; 16 rejected rows had the surname of an SPWS starter, most of them a first name in another form. The user decided the first-name question (Q1 A: one identity entry per event) and signed off 11 events. They are committed on LOCAL with 419 results, every one carrying its printed federation where the source has a country, and 199 season nationalities with no conflict. The two Criteriums and Faches wait for sign-off. CERT and PROD follow the LOCAL commit, event by event, machine-checked against it.
- **Tests.** ADM.ID.01–07 and ADM.ID.10 (pytest: the three steps, the four paths, exact identity), ADM.ID.08 (PENDING listed and blocking sign-off), ADM.ID.09 (pgTAP: `fn_spws_starter_ids`), ADM.ID.11 (an override identity entry confirms its alias pair at sign-off), ADM.ID.12 and P3.OV19–20 (an entry names its fencer, resolved per environment). NAT.EVID.01 (the fold), NAT.EVID.02–03 (pgTAP: draft commit and ingest function carry the column), NAT.EVID.04 (the promotion copies it).

LEAHEY's stored results carry the scraped name "167". The roster spells one fencer "ANNA-LISE Mion", in reversed order.

## Amendment (2026-10-03) — why the year before the season's end year is accepted

**Decided by the user on 2026-10-03**, with [ADR-047](047-vcat-invariant-trigger-and-splitter-consolidation.md)'s amendment of the same day (V2 A on `doc/plans/category-check-seven-results-2026-10-03.html`).

§1 accepts, for identity, a birth year that fits the bracket's category in the year before the season's end year, "because EVF categories go by calendar year and an autumn event falls in it". The rule stands; its stated reason was too broad. The stored results show two practices:

- **The EVF circuit followed the season's end year in autumn 2025.** KORONA Przemysław and KROCHMALSKI Jakub, born 1976, fenced V2 at the EVF circuit events of 20 September and 1 November 2025, and at every other 2025/26 event.
- **The World Championships followed the event's calendar year.** At the championships of 12–16 November 2025, the same two fencers were in V1, at 49 in 2025.

The accepted year therefore covers events that count age by their own calendar year, as the World Championships did. The category check now accepts the same year for a result carrying its source's label (ADR-047 amendment), so the identity rule and the check agree.
