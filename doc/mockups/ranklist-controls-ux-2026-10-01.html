Every control in one predictable place

The list and the drilldown share the same controls — language, the Ranking | PPW view, the ODS download and, in the drilldown, close — and the drilldown has one number that matters most: the total. Proposal: put the total at the top, say it once, and give every control a fixed place.

Today

Where the controls and the total are now

Screenshots from LOCAL, 1 October 2026, with the switch flag on.

  1. The total is the most important number, and it is the hardest to find. It sits under the charts, in a line that repeats the SPWS and EVF+ sums the chart headings already show, and on a phone it breaks across lines mid-phrase.
  2. The view switch has no fixed home. On the list it sits among the filters under „Widok”. In the drilldown it hangs on the end of the rank text, and on a narrow screen it drops to its own line while the ODS button drops to another.
  3. The two switches look identical — same grey frame, same blue — but do different things: one changes the language of the page, the other changes the data.
  4. The flags are emoji, and Windows does not draw emoji flags. Chrome and Edge on Windows show the letters „GB” and „PL” in boxes instead of the flags.
  5. The ODS button is an unlabelled glyph (⎙). It does what it should — downloads the ODS spreadsheet — but nothing on it says „ODS”.
  6. On a phone the view switch stretches into an empty box on the list, and in the drilldown the close button and the switch scroll away with the results.
  7. Keyboard and screen readers: Esc does not close the drilldown; neither switch says which option is selected; the drilldown is announced in English only („Fencer details”).

Drilldown

1 Top-right corner — the frame. What belongs to the window: the language, and in the drilldown the close button. Always the same spot.

2 The headline — place and total. In the drilldown, directly under the name: the fencer’s place and total in the largest figures on the screen, said once. The SPWS and EVF+ parts are not repeated; they stay where they already are, in the chart headings. The line of sums under the charts is gone.

3 Right end of the same line — the data controls. The Ranking | PPW view, then the ODS download, in that order, in the same place on the list and in the drilldown. Switching the view changes the headline next to it, and the ODS file is the view you see.

Everything below the header stays exactly as built: the bars, the legend, both tables in their framed panels with the greyed rows, the phone cards, and every tournament name that has a results URL is a link that opens the results in a new tab, with the full tournament code as its tooltip.

Alternatives considered

Other ways, and why not

B · Everything in one cluster, top right

Ranking | PPW, ODS, language and ✕ side by side in the title row. One row fewer — but it mixes window controls with data controls, and on a 375 px phone it does not fit next to a long name.

C · A „⋯” menu for ODS and language

Only the view switch stays visible. The tidiest look — but it hides two things people use, costs an extra tap, and „⋯” is not obvious to many of our older users.

D · The total in the title row, beside the name

As high as it can go — but the name, the total, the language and ✕ then compete in one line, and on a phone the total gets pushed under the name anyway. In A the total sits one line lower, where it has room to be the largest figure and stands next to the switch that changes it.
