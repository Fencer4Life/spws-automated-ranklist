Public ranklist · season rules · UX proposal · 1 October 2026

The chosen season’s ranking rules, one click from the list

A pill pinned to the bottom of the screen names the season the list shows — „Reguły rankingu na sezon 2026/2027” — and opens a modal with that season’s buckets: which results count and how they add up to the columns of the table. Pick another season in the dropdown and the link and its rules switch with it. Everything is drawn from the season’s own definition, so it cannot drift from the ranking.

Revision 2 · 1 October · what changed

The pill stays at the bottom of the screen

The pill „ⓘ Reguły rankingu na sezon 2026/2027” leaves the grey strip under the filters. It now floats at the bottom of the visible screen and stays there while the list scrolls under it. The strip goes back to showing only „kat. 1 — roczniki: …”. Everything else, including the modal, the season switching and the ★, is unchanged.

How the app finds the bottom of the screen

  • The browser does it. The pill is pinned with CSS (position: fixed; bottom: 16px) to the visible screen itself, not to the page. Nothing is measured with JavaScript, and it holds on every screen size.
  • iPhone: it keeps above the home bar (env(safe-area-inset-bottom)). When Safari shows or hides its toolbar, the pill moves with the visible bottom.
  • Nothing hides behind it. The list gets about 72 px of extra space at its end. The last row, and the CT/PD switch on CERT, scroll clear of the pill.
  • It stays out of the way. The menu drawer and every modal cover it. It shows only on the ranklist, and only for a season with stored rules (2023/24 has none).
  • WordPress: the pill behaves the same way on the public site. One thing will be checked on the live page before building: a WordPress theme that puts a transform on a box around the ranklist would pin the pill to that box instead of the screen.

Decided: the middle of the bottom edge APPROVED 1 OCT

Try both with the switch above the demo. Middle (recommended) is equally easy to reach with either thumb on a phone, and on a computer it sits over the names, not the numbers. Right corner would sit over the „Razem” column, the one people read.

The parts

Each element, and why it looks the way it does

ElementWhat it isWhy
The pillA blue pill pinned to the bottom of the screen: ⓘ „Reguły rankingu na sezon 2026/2027”. The season in it is the one selected in the dropdown, and it changes the moment the dropdown does. It stays on the bottom edge while the list scrolls (revision 2).It is always in reach. However far down the list a visitor has scrolled, the rules of the season they are looking at are one tap away. It leaves the control row (switch, ODS) for controls, it reads as a question a visitor has, and naming the season makes plain which rules these are.
The titleThe same words as the link, then one line: the season code, whether it is the active season, and that the rules hold in every weapon, gender and category.The modal is about the season, not about the list’s filters; saying so stops a visitor wondering whether épée or V1 has other rules.
The sumBoxes in the drilldown headline’s style that repeat the table’s columns: SPWS + EVF+ = Razem, each with its short rule. A PPW-only season has one box, Punkty.A visitor reads the table first; the sum explains its columns in the table’s own words.
The two cardsSPWS and EVF+, in the colours of the drilldown’s charts (blue, amber). One row per bucket: the types, „2 najlepsze wyniki” or „każdy wynik — zawsze wliczany”, and one ★ per counted result, in the card’s colour — MPW too. The number of ★ comes from the chosen season’s rules: 2 for PPW in 2026/27, 4 in 2025/26 and 2024/25.The ★ is the same mark the drilldown puts on a counted result, so the rules and the fencer’s breakdown speak one language. The full names under the chips cover visitors who do not know „PSW”.
Who is rankedFrom the season’s entry_types: only a PPW or MPW start brings a fencer into the ranking.It explains the most common surprise: a fencer with EVF results only is not on the list.
Rolling rankingShown for the active season only: until this season’s event has taken place, the result from its counterpart in the previous season counts (↩), never both.It explains the ↩ rows in the drilldown.
CoefficientsThe type coefficients of the shown types, from the public fn_public_scoring_params.They are part of the season’s definition and explain why an MPW result is worth more. Optional: see the question.
The footer„Jak liczone są punkty za jedno miejsce → Tabela punktacji”, and a line tying the ★ to the drilldown.The algorithm stays in the annex; this modal stays about buckets and sums.

Data

Drawn from the chosen season’s definition

Source, for the season in the dropdownWhat it shows
json_ranking_rules.domesticThe SPWS card, one row per bucket.
json_ranking_rules.internationalThe EVF+ card — only when the season publishes the full ranking.
tbl_season.enum_ranking_publication = PPW_ONLYOnly the SPWS card; the sum is one box, „Punkty”; the note says only the PPW ranking is published. (2025/26 and 2024/25 in the mock.)
json_ranking_rules.entry_types„Kto jest w rankingu”; hidden when the season has none.
tbl_season.bool_activeThe rolling-ranking line.
fn_public_scoring_params(season)The coefficients of the types shown.
No stored rules (2023/24)The link is hidden: the modal never states rules the season does not store.

The page already reads the rules for the drilldown (fetchRankingRules) and re-reads them on every season change; the modal reuses that, so it needs no new database access.

Alternatives

Other ways, and why not

  1. B · A „?” button beside ODS. Compact, but an icon alone is easy to miss, it cannot name the season, and it adds a fourth control to a row we just made calm.
  2. C · Click the column headers (SPWS, EVF+, Razem). Nobody expects help behind a header, and a phone has no hover to hint at it.
  3. D · A rules panel always shown above the table. Read once, needed rarely; it would push the list down on every visit.
