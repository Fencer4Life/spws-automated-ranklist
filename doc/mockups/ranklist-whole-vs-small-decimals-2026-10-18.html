My view. If the decimals are noise, A removes the noise; the cost is the pairs that look tied and the sums that miss by one, and the drilldown answers both. B keeps the information for close calls and makes the columns line up, but every number still carries a tail, so the list is quieter, not clean. B′ is the strongest form of B.

C takes the best of both: the list reads like A, and a decimal appears only where it carries meaning, exactly where A would otherwise show two different ranks with the same number. Its costs are small: a row’s look depends on its neighbours, those few rows break the right edge by a small tail, and there is one more rule to test. The sums still miss by one, as in A. I now lean to C.
