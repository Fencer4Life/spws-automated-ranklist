Reading an international listing that names two categories

EVF fences direct elimination in each category; only its pools are joined, and a category of fewer than two fencers joins the nearer younger category’s DE. So an FTL listing named for two categories at an EVF event is a joint pool round, whose categories have their own DE, or a younger category’s DE that a lone older fencer joined. Our international reader refuses the whole event on either. This plan proposes reading both as EVF fences them.

What changed since rev 1

The premise was wrong

Rev 1 read these listings as brackets that join two categories

Rev 1 (2 Nov) saw that EVF’s results API publishes each such pair as two categories, each with its own places and N, and proposed taking those categories from EVF’s API. You pointed out (3 Nov) that EVF has no mixed-category direct elimination: under the EVF Circuit Handbook v6.3 (August 2026), after the poules each fencer fences DE in their own category when it has at least two fencers; a category with fewer joins the nearer younger category for the DE; and both semi-final losers are third. The categories were fenced apart all along, so their results are in FTL already. Rev 1’s C1 (read EVF’s API), C3 and C4 are withdrawn. C2 stands, signed B.

Why our reader refuses them

  • By name, before reading. international_sources._ftl_schedule refuses a listing whose name parses to two categories (_refuse_uncategorised) before fetching its data, so it never learns whether the listing is a pool round. One such listing refuses the whole event (ADR-119 G60’s SourceRefused).
  • The DE sub-event rule is a domestic one. scrape_ftl_event_urls._skip_reason skips “Cat 3 DE” as a sub-event whose parent is the result. At an SPWS event the parent of a joint pool is the result, split by birth year (ADR-049, ADR-076). At an EVF event the sub-events are the results.
  • Pool rounds are dropped by name only on this route. The domestic FTL path also drops a round whose results page has no tableau (ADR-076 rule 1, ADR-067’s structural rule); the international path does not.
  • What it costs. 4 of the 29 international events of 30 months have such a listing, and an open event with one is refused whole. By EVF’s country column, none of the three categories PROD lacks had a Polish entrant.
