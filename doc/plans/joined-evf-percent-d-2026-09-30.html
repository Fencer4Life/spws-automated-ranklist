Chosen rule · 30 September 2026 · EVF × (1 + 0.05 · d), at least EVF + d · checked on every order and on three seasons

The chosen rule: EVF × (1 + 0.05 · d), at least EVF + d

The youngest category of a joined bracket gets pure EVF. An older fencer gets 5% per category step, and never less than EVF + d. Nobody gets more than the fencer placed ahead minus 1 point. The rule keeps Z1–Z4 in every order tested and in every real bracket of 2023/24–2025/26. It never rewards finishing lower. Every older fencer of a small category gains at least 0.38 points over fencing alone. The limits that remain are shown with an example each in §5.

Decided
  • A bracket of 1–3 fencers is a meeting, not a competition, and scores 3 / 2 / 1.
  • A joined bracket of 4–15:
    • the youngest category gets pure EVF;
    • an older category gets EVF × (1 + 0.05 · d), but at least EVF + d;
    • everyone is capped at 1 point under the fencer placed ahead.
  • A single-category bracket or a bracket of 16+ scores plain EVF.
  • The joining rules, including the exception for a small category at the young end, are unchanged.
  • No bottom bonus, no +1 for last place, no tie clause.

1 · Reguła punktacji

Reguła punktacji

Stawka to wszyscy zawodnicy jednej listy wyników. N to liczba zawodników w stawce, a p to miejsce w stawce. Każde miejsce jest rozstrzygnięte walką, więc w stawce nie ma miejsc ex aequo.

  1. Stawka 1–3 zawodników to spotkanie, nie zawody: N − p + 1 pkt, czyli 3, 2, 1.
  2. Stawka jednej kategorii wiekowej albo stawka 16 i więcej zawodników: punkty EVF za miejsce w stawce.
  3. Stawka łączona 4–15 zawodników:
    • zawodnik najmłodszej kategorii w stawce dostaje punkty EVF za miejsce w całej stawce;
    • zawodnik starszej kategorii dostaje punkty EVF × (1 + 0,05 · d), ale co najmniej EVF + d. d to różnica między jego kategorią a najmłodszą kategorią w stawce; na przykład w stawce z V0 zawodnik V4 ma d = 4;
    • nikt nie dostaje więcej niż zawodnik, który zajął miejsce przed nim, minus 1 pkt.
  4. Wynik mnoży się przez współczynnik rangi zawodów (PPW 1,0; MPW 1,2).

Punkty EVF = 50 − 49 · ln p / ln N + 10 · liczba wygranych rund eliminacji + premia za podium (3 / 2 / 1 · 3 · ∛N).

How it reads

The two parts meet at exactly 20 EVF points, whatever d is: 5% × d × 20 = d.

  • Above 20 EVF points, the older fencer gets 5% per step.
  • Below 20, they get + d.

In a bracket of 8, places 1–4 have EVF above 20 and places 5–8 below it. So the top gets a premium that grows with the score, and the bottom gets a fixed 1–4 points.

2 · Zasady łączenia kategorii (bez zmian)

Zasady łączenia
  1. Kategoria, w której startuje mniej niż 4 zawodników, jest automatycznie łączona z sąsiednią kategorią wiekową, czyli z najbliższą kategorią obecną w tej broni i płci.
  2. W jednej stawce łączy się najwyżej 2 kategorie.
  3. Więcej niż 2 kategorie łączy się tylko wtedy, gdy inaczej nie da się połączyć wszystkich kategorii poniżej 4 zawodników albo stawka miałaby mniej niż 4 zawodników.
  4. Kategoria z co najmniej 4 zawodnikami może startować osobno.
  5. Kategoria z co najmniej 4 zawodnikami może być w stawce łączonej tylko najmłodszą kategorią. Wyjątek: jeśli najmłodsza obecna kategoria ma mniej niż 4 zawodników i nawet razem z kolejnymi kategoriami poniżej 4 zawodników nie osiąga 4, łączy się je z najbliższą starszą kategorią, także gdy ma ona 4 lub więcej zawodników.
  6. Jeśli w całej broni i płci startuje mniej niż 4 zawodników, wszystkie obecne kategorie tworzą jedną stawkę.

3 · Z1–Z4

Zasady
  1. Z1. Zawodnicy najmłodszej kategorii dostają czyste punkty EVF.
  2. Z2. Kategoria, która ma 1–3 zawodników, nigdy nie traci na łączeniu w porównaniu z turniejem samej swojej kategorii. Zapłacić może tylko kategoria, która ma co najmniej 4 zawodników i mogłaby startować osobno.
  3. Z3. Nikt nie dostaje więcej punktów niż ktokolwiek, kto zajął miejsce przed nim.
  4. Z4. Nikt nie ma tyle samo punktów co zawodnik na miejscu przed nim. Ma zawsze co najmniej 1 pkt mniej.

Method: every distinct finishing order was scored in two sets. The first covers two categories 1–4 steps apart, up to 15 fencers (261,984 orders), plus three categories of 4–8 and five categories of 5–7 (43,704 orders). The second is the 109 real joined brackets of 4–15 fencers from every PPW and MPW of 2023/24–2025/26, keeping each category's real internal order; there, orders are counted as equally likely, and again with younger categories 2× stronger. All scores are before the rank coefficient.

Why it cannot fail:

  • For 4–15 fencers, neighbouring EVF places are at least 1.25 points apart. So the cap can only trim part of an older fencer's premium: it never takes anyone below EVF and never reaches the youngest category.
  • An older fencer always scores above EVF, and EVF is always at least 3 / 2 / 1 for their place in their own category.

5 · What remains, with an example for each point

Two assumptions used below

Past results only give places inside each category. They do not show who would have finished ahead across categories, so two cases are tested:

  • No idea who is stronger: every finishing order is equally likely.
  • Younger fencers stronger: a fencer one category younger is twice as likely to finish ahead.

B1 · An older winner can score more than any stand-alone winner, but it is rare

An older fencer who wins a joined bracket gets EVF for 1st place plus 5% per step. Once in a while, that is more than any winner of a stand-alone category got in three seasons.

Going above 124.02 needs a large bracket with a V0 in it and an older fencer in 1st place. Across all real brackets of three seasons, we would expect that less than once in total (0.4 times).

A category of 3 is joined; a category of 4 fences alone. Take V3 with 5 fencers. The best V4 fencer's score depends on whether V4 has 3 fencers (joined with V3) or 4 (alone):

What it means: with 3 fencers, the best V4 scores more than the stand-alone 84.29 only by finishing ahead of every V3 fencer. One more V4 entry changes their range from "8.78 to 102.90" to "always 84.29". This comes from joining itself; the 5% premium adds only 4.90 points at the top.

B3 · Who else is in the bracket decides d

d counts the steps from the youngest category present. The same V4 fencer in the same place scores more when a younger category is in the bracket.

V4 fencer in a bracket of 8Youngest in the bracket is V3 (d = 1)Youngest in the bracket is V0 (d = 4)
2nd place (EVF 65.67)65.67 × 1.05 = 68.9565.67 × 1.20 = 78.80

Real case: in PPW2-2025-2026 Foil M, one V0 fencer entered, so the V4 fencer had d = 4. Without that V0 fencer, the youngest category would have been V1 and d would have been 3.

What it means: depending on who else entered, the same result is worth up to 14.7 points more in 1st place (102.90 against 117.60), 9.85 more in 2nd, and 3 more in last place. Most older fencers have d = 1 (250 of 381 results); d = 4 happened 12 times.

What it means: in a category's season ranking, "best of my category that day" is worth more at events where that category was big. It comes from joining under any rule, not from the premium.

In an exception bracket it is the older category that loses. With V0 3 + V1 4 and the three V0 fencers ahead, the V1 winner scores 26.35 against 84.29 alone.

What it means: in three seasons, 72 real fencers would on average lose points against fencing alone. A youngest category of 4+ ends below its own tournament in 40% of its results, or 24% if younger fencers are stronger. This is by design (Z2): only a category that could fence alone can lose.

7 · Question

Is this the final rule for the implementation plan?

No design question is open. The implementation plan from 29 September describes an earlier design, so it has to be rewritten. The new plan will cover:

  • a new ADR;
  • the scoring engine in SQL, replacing the place-and-medal engine;
  • the joining rules and the exception in ingestion;
  • the 3 / 2 / 1 meeting bracket;
  • the calculator and the public scoring table;
  • tests on these examples;
  • the documentation gate.
  1. Yes: rewrite the implementation plan for this rulerecommended. §5 lists no fault in the rule itself. B1 and B6 are rare, B2 and B4 come with joining under any rule, B3 is at most 14.7 points, and B5 is by design.
  2. Not yet: name what should change first.
