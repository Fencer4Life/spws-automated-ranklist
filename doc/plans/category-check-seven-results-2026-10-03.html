✓ V2 A The three World Championships results: what the check should say about them

KORONA Przemysław and KROCHMALSKI Jakub, both born 1976, fenced V1 at the World Championships of 12–16 November 2025. SPWS counts them as V2 for 2025/26. Both facts are right. The Championships count age in the calendar year of the event (49 in 2025), while SPWS counts it in the season's end year (50 in 2026). ADR-106 already accepts this shift when it identifies international results. The check was never taught it.

  • A (recommended): keep the results and the ranking as they are, and teach the check the same shift. A result that carries its source's label passes when that label equals the category for the calendar year in which the event started. These three pass. LEAHEY's 2024/25 rows would still fail under 1976 (48 and 49 are V1 in either year), so the change hides nothing that is wrong. This needs a migration for the view, pgTAP, and an amendment to ADR-047 and ADR-106. It would be one small release.
  • B: leave the check as it is. The three stay listed permanently, as known.
  • C: a new ranking rule, under which a result earned in another category's bracket does not count. KROCHMALSKI would drop from 5th to 6th in the live 2026/27 rolling V2 men's sabre ranking. Not recommended: the result is his, from the bracket the Championships required him to fence in.

2 · Why they were stored, and why they are still listed

The check and the guard disagree since May

  • The guard on every result insert is trg_assert_result_vcat. Since the ADR-056 revision (migration 20260503000009), it skips the comparison when a result carries its source's label. The reason is written in the migration: the organiser's bracket is the source of truth for a past tournament. All seven carry a label, so all seven were accepted.
  • The check is the view vw_vcat_violation (ADR-047, migration 20260430000001). It was written four days before that revision and never changed. It still compares the bracket with the birth-year category and ignores the label, so it lists every labelled result the guard deliberately let through.

So "violation" overstates it. The view marks a disagreement. What the disagreement means differs between the two groups below.

3 · Group A · KORONA, KROCHMALSKI · three results

A one-year shift at the World Championships

  • KORONA Przemysław (#139): 89 results. 87 fit 1976 under the SPWS rule:
    • V1 throughout 2023/24 (32) and 2024/25 (27);
    • V2 throughout 2025/26 (28), including EVF circuit events on 20 Sep and 1 Nov 2025.
    The exceptions are his two World Championships results, V1 on 12–16 Nov 2025.
  • KROCHMALSKI Jakub (#156): 18 results, with the same pattern. V1 in 2023/24 (7) and 2024/25 (5), V2 in 2025/26 (5), and one exception: the World Championships, V1.

Born 1976, both turned 49 in 2025 and 50 in 2026. The World Championships placed them by the event's calendar year (V1). SPWS places them by the season's end year (V2). Their birth years are right. Nothing in the stored data is wrong.

ADR-106 §1 already allows for this. When it identifies an international row, "the year before the season's end year is accepted". The check was not given the same allowance.

The ADR-106 wording needs a correction. It attributes the calendar-year rule to EVF. These two fencers' EVF circuit results in autumn 2025 follow the season rule (V2 on 20 Sep and 1 Nov). Only the World Championships follow the calendar year. The wording should be corrected when ADR-106 is amended.

4 · What the seven do to rankings

One of them decides a place in a live ranking

The ranking files every result under the fencer's category for that season, from the birth year, and never under the bracket's label (fn_ranking_full's two matching functions, migration 20260919000008, lines 341–344, 363 and 483). A result is scored from the bracket in which it was fenced.
