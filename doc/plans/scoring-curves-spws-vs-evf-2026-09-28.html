    How the fit works

    Fit to EVF chooses złoto, srebro, brąz, 4th place and points per fencer below so that the SPWS value of every place, in every field from 4 up to the hand-over, lands as close as possible to the EVF value (least squares, each place-and-field cell weighted equally). Field points stay at log₂ N and fields of 1–3 keep their table. The medal-growth choice changes what the fit can reach. The fitted numbers are shown to one decimal; a regulation would round them.
