Draft · Status: Proposed

Amendment to ADR-106 (2026-10-17): FIE events, a fencer entered by Poland counts without an SPWS start

Context

At an FIE event each national federation enters its fencers, at most 4 per individual bracket. At Tbilisi 2026 no federation exceeded 4 in any of the 24 brackets, and all 1,386 rows printed a federation. A row printed POL at an FIE event is therefore a fencer the Polish federation entered. Under §1 such a fencer with no PPW or MPW result is PENDING. At Tbilisi that held back SZUMIELEWICZ Paweł (2nd of 62, Foil M V1) and KOSZYK Agnieszka (12th of 45, Sabre W V1). Both are in the fencer table through administrator migrations. The PZSz admission already counts a fencer who is in the table without an SPWS start (ADR-111).

Decision

  1. Scope. Tournament types MSW and PSW, including the IMSW code (D1). PEW, MEW and IMEW (EVF) keep §1 unchanged.
  2. A POL row is stored by identity alone. At an FIE event, a row printed POL that is exactly one roster fencer by §1’s identity (surname, first given name, a birth year fitting the category in the season’s end year or the year before) is stored, whether or not the fencer has a PPW or MPW result. Through ADR-110 §2 the fencer is then ranked.
  3. Recorded aliases count (D3 A). At an FIE event, identity also accepts a recorded, unrevoked alias (json_name_aliases minus json_revoked_aliases) in place of the canonical surname and first name. The birth year must still fit, and a row not printed POL still needs an SPWS start.
  4. Everything else as §1. A POL row that is not exactly one roster fencer is PENDING, and no fencer is created (ADR-020, D2 A). A row not printed POL needs an SPWS start. Everything else is rejected.
  5. Rule 1 (D5 A). The staging summary flags an FIE bracket where one federation has more than 4 rows. It does not refuse it.

Alternatives considered

  • Override entries per event. Rejected: you would have to push every one.
  • Create a fencer for any POL row. Rejected: FTL publishes no birth year, so the category cannot be confirmed, and ADR-020 and ADR-110 forbid it.
  • Drop the SPWS-start condition for every international event, EVF included. Rejected: not asked for. EVF entries are not federation entries, and the country column has proven unreliable there.
  • Select the rule by organizer rather than type. Not chosen. Every caller of admit() already has the type, while the organizer is not available there, and _organizer_for_event reports IMSW as UNKNOWN (ADR-105 §5.2). FIE runs exactly MSW and PSW (ADR-008), and on LOCAL the FIE organizer row (id 3) holds only IMSW and MSW events.

Consequences

  • international_admission.admit() takes the tournament type. Its three callers pass it: stages._admit_international_rows (python/pipeline/stages.py:118), resolve_tournament_results (python/matcher/pipeline.py:216) and the EVF sync (python/scrapers/evf_sync.py:897, which only ever sees EVF types).
  • ADM_ID_04 (POL without an SPWS start is PENDING) stays true for EVF events. New tests ADM.FIE.01–10 cover FIE.
  • Documentation updates: ADR-110 Context and §1 wording; ADR-018 line 71; RTM FR-09, FR-152 and INTL.POL; spec §8.5 and Appendix C; the handbook pages subsystems/ingestion.html, domain/competition-and-ranking.html and operations/operator-runbooks.html, plus subsystems/identity-and-recompute.html after a passage check.
