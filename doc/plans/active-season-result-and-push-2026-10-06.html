04 · Limits

What this does not prove

  • CERT and PROD are untouched. The migration has run only on LOCAL, twice: once incrementally and once on a fresh reset. CERT runs it on the push.

  • „Today“ is the database’s UTC date, as before. A season starting on 1 August becomes active at 00:00 UTC, which is 02:00 in Warsaw.
