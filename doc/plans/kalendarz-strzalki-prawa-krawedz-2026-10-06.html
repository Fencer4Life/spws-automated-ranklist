Flush right only on the narrowest phones

The approved mock placed the buttons at left: min(100% − 36px, 300px). That puts them at the right edge only while the drum is at most 336 px wide. On anything wider they stopped 300 px from the left. The first sheet even said so (“at most 300 px from the left”), and I did not flag it against your rule: the right side. No test pinned the horizontal position, so nothing caught it.
