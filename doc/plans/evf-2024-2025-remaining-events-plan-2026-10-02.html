The eight 2024/25 EVF events never repaired

Every 2025/26 international event is repaired on LOCAL, CERT and PROD. Eight 2024/25 records still hold the old EVF import: no source link, no printed federation, and N equal to the number of Poles stored. This plan finds each one’s organiser source, corrects the records that block a source, and takes the events through the same per-event review and machine-checked rollout. The seed refresh follows it.

N Guildford 2025’s source names itself differently

The FTL schedule 9CC8D1BC… is titled “BVF 6 Weapon International 2025”, 4–5 January 2025, épée, foil and sabre. Its dates and weapons pass the check; its title does not say Guildford, so the tool needs a person to confirm the name (it never accepts a confirmed date). EVF’s calendar lists EVF Circuit – Guildford (GBR) on 4 January 2025, run by British Veterans Fencing.

  • A confirm: the BVF 6 Weapon International 2025 is EVF Circuit Guildford 2025.
  • B do not confirm; Guildford 2025 stays unrepaired.

Recommendation: A.

W Two records list fewer weapons than the event had

The runner skips a bracket whose weapon the event does not list. Fâches 2025 (PEW5s, sabre only) also had foil, with 1 SPWS starter; Stockholm 2025 (PEW13e, épée only) also had foil, with 3.

  • A add the missing weapon to the event and rename its code (PEW5fs, PEW13ef). No tool renames an event that has results; the code is in tournament codes, override files and both cloud copies.
  • B add the missing weapon and keep the code: the letters then understate the weapons, nothing else changes.
  • C leave both records as they are: those 4 foil results stay out.

Recommendation: B. The code is a label; scoring and the rankings do not read its letters.

J Jabłonna 2025’s foil is a separate record

EVF counts Warsaw-Legionowo (Jabłonna) 2025 as one event in three weapons. Our épée and sabre are PEW7es-2024-2025 (repaired), the foil is PEW8f-2024-2025, dated 30 March to 12 April, with N of 2–6 (22 results).

  • A fold the foil into one event: rename PEW7es to PEW7efs, re-stage all three weapons from its FTL schedule, delete PEW8f.
  • B keep PEW8f as the foil event: correct its dates to 29–30 March, give it the same FTL schedule (the runner takes the foil brackets only) and re-stage it.

Recommendation: B. The rankings are per weapon, so two records for one weekend score exactly as one; B renames nothing that is already released and verified.

X PEW15f-2024-2025 is not an EVF event

Two foil results on 15 May 2025 (KORONA 3rd of 5 in V1, BAZAK 1st of 7 in V2), no source, and no EVF circuit event that day. If you know what this tournament was, say so and it gets a source like the others.

  • A leave it untouched in this pass.
  • B delete it and its two results.

Recommendation: A, until its source is known; deleting results is not reversible.

The work · no new code expected

Steps

  1. Find the two missing sources (Budapest 2024, Stockholm 2025) and confirm Terni’s 4fence address. If one is not found, that event stays out and is reported.
  2. Correct the records on LOCAL: EVF name, city and country where blank (needed for the name check); the weapon added for Fâches and Stockholm (W); PEW8f’s dates (J).
  3. Event links through set_event_source_urls, the Guildford name confirmed per N.
  4. Stage on LOCAL and review: one page in the same layout, with the EVF cross-check per event.
  5. Your sign-off per event, override files for linked or excluded rows, commit on LOCAL.
  6. Release, scripts/preflight.sh exit 0 first; pgTAP data tests that pin these events accept both states until the seed refresh.
  7. CERT, then PROD: the same record corrections and links by scoped updates that return what changed; each event through the runner, one at a time, machine-checked against LOCAL.
  8. Seed refresh from PROD (ADR-036: the full suite passes before the pointer moves), then drop the pgTAP seed alternatives.
