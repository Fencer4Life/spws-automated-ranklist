Scoring family · engine · Points Table · Calculator · season · plan · 3 October 2026

The engine, the Points Table and the Calculator change together, per season

You asked whether the three are tied to the season by version, naming or anything else. Partly. One engine code that names its season ties them, and the season lock freezes all three at once. But nothing declares them a group, nothing stops a released engine from being edited in place, and nothing checks at season rollover that the pages know the new season. This plan adds that contract: one manifest, fingerprints of what was released, and checks in preflight, CI and release.

02 · What is missing

Four gaps between "tied by a name" and "kept and changed as a group"

G1 Nothing declares the group

Which SQL function, which TypeScript function, which golden values, which pages and which seasons belong to an engine is spread across five ADRs and the handbook. No file states it and no check reads it. Adding an engine means remembering every place.

G2 A released engine can be edited in place

"Released engines are immutable" is written in ADR-097, ADR-104 and the handbook, but no check enforces it. A later migration could CREATE OR REPLACE fn_score_spws_evf_joined_v1_2026_2027 and update scoring.test.ts in the same commit. Every gate would stay green, and a rescore of a locked season would no longer reproduce its stored results.

The place-and-medal engine was released on 28 Sep and deleted on 30 Sep (ADR-104). That was legitimate, because nothing had been scored with it. Today nothing tells that case apart from deleting an engine that has.

G3 Season rollover is not checked

The calculator follows the active season (SEASON_CODE = null). When 2027/2028 becomes active by date (ADR-031) with an engine the pages do not implement, visitors see "silnik … nie jest silnikiem Załącznika nr 1". It fails closed, but the public discovers it first.

G4 The annex's address carries no season

The annex source is doc/tools/Tabela-punktacji-SPWS_2026-2027.html, pinned with SEASON_CODE = 'SPWS-2026-2027' (L845), but it is published as tabela-punktacji.html. A 2027/2028 annex would have to overwrite it at the same URL. The rules of a season that is locked, and still counts in carry-over, would disappear from the public.

03 · The proposal

One declared scoring family per engine, checked everywhere

A scoring family is one engine code together with everything that must agree with it: its SQL strategy, its TypeScript implementation, its golden values, the pages that compute with it, and the seasons assigned to it. Each family is declared once, and the checks read that declaration.

M1 · The manifest: scoring/families.json

{ "engine": "SPWS_EVF_JOINED_V1_2026_2027",
  "status": "released",                         draft → released → retired
  "sql": { "strategy": "fn_score_spws_evf_joined_v1_2026_2027",
           "fingerprint": "md5 of pg_get_functiondef" },
  "ts":  { "symbol": "JOINED_ENGINE" },
  "golden": { "file": "frontend/tests/golden/SPWS_EVF_JOINED_V1_2026_2027.json",
              "sha256": "…" },
  "seasons": ["SPWS-2026-2027"],
  "annex": { "SPWS-2026-2027": "tabela-punktacji-2026-2027.html" } }

The manifest holds no formula and no parameters. Parameters stay in the season configuration, edited in Admin and frozen by the lock, exactly as today.

M2 · Released means frozen, mechanically

  • SQL fingerprint. A pgTAP file generated from the manifest asserts md5(pg_get_functiondef(strategy)) for every released family. A one-character edit to a released strategy turns CI red.
  • Golden values frozen. scoring.test.ts splits its expectations into one file per engine. The manifest stores each file's hash, so editing a released family's expected values is caught.
  • Append-only. A preflight and CI check compares the manifest with origin/main. A released entry may not change; it may only move to retired, and only with an adr reference. A formula change is therefore always a new engine code, as ADR-097 already requires.

M3 · The pages take their engine and season from the manifest

  • build-scoring-pages.mjs writes each page's ENGINE and SEASON_CODE constants from the manifest instead of hand-written literals. The calculator accepts any released family that scoring.ts implements, not one fixed constant.
  • Each season gets its own annex: tabela-punktacji-2026-2027.html. tabela-punktacji.html stays, as a byte-identical copy of the current season's annex, so every existing link keeps working. ADR-102 §08 forbids moving a published path. The annex of a locked season stays published unchanged.

M4 · Rollover is checked before the public sees it

  • In CI (pgTAP). Every active row of tbl_scoring_engine has a released family, and every released family has its row. An admin can only select engines from that table, so an admin can never assign an engine the pages lack.
  • In CI (pgTAP). Every engine assigned to any season in the seed, as the season default or per tournament type, is a released family listing that season.
  • At release and daily (CERT and PROD). A read-only check calls fn_public_scoring_params for the active season and for the next season if it exists. If either season has no family or no annex in the manifest, it sends a Telegram alert. This is the only live part, and it uses the anon key.

04 · Walk-through

The 2027/2028 season, step by step

  1. June 2027: the board changes the formula. A developer adds fn_score_spws_evf_joined_v2_2027_2028 and its registry row. CI fails at once: the registry row has no family (M4). The developer adds the family as draft, together with its TypeScript implementation, golden values derived from pgTAP, and tabela-punktacji-2027-2028.html.
  2. Release. The family becomes released, and its fingerprint and golden hash are written. From now on it is frozen (M2).
  3. On CERT, an admin creates 2027/2028 and assigns the new engine to PPW and MPW. The daily check finds the family and its annex: no alert. If the admin had chosen an engine without an annex, the alert would arrive the same day, not when the season starts.
  4. 1 September 2027: the season becomes active by date. The calculator follows it and computes with V2. A release switches tabela-punktacji.html to the 2027/2028 annex; the daily check flags a release that has not happened yet. tabela-punktacji-2026-2027.html stays online unchanged.
  5. Adjacent path: someone "fixes" V1 in place. The edited strategy changes its fingerprint, so the generated pgTAP fails. Changing the fingerprint in the manifest fails the append-only check. The only way forward is a new engine code.
  6. Adjacent path: an engine released but never used, as place-and-medal was. It moves to retired with its ADR reference. Its entry stays in the manifest as the record of what was released.

07 · ADR draft · Status: Proposed

ADR-107: A scoring family is declared once and released as a frozen group

Context. The scoring engine (SQL), the Points Table (annex) and the Points Calculator compute one formula. ADR-102 gave them one module and one parameter source, and ADR-097 freezes a season's configuration on its first score. Their grouping is implicit: it rests on a shared engine code. Immutability of a released engine is stated, not enforced. Rollover to a new season is not checked, and the annex's published address carries no season.

Decision.

  1. A scoring family is declared in scoring/families.json: one engine code, its SQL strategy and fingerprint, its TypeScript symbol, its golden file and hash, its seasons and each season's annex.
  2. A released family is frozen. Generated pgTAP checks the SQL fingerprint, Vitest checks the golden hash, and a preflight and CI check keeps the manifest append-only against origin/main. A family may only be retired, with an ADR reference. A formula change is a new engine code (restating ADR-097).
  3. The published pages take their engine and season from the manifest. Each season has its own annex; tabela-punktacji.html stays the current season's copy.
  4. Every active engine row has a released family. A daily read-only check on CERT and PROD alerts on Telegram when the active or next season lacks a family or an annex.

Alternatives. Registry columns as the declaration; fingerprint only; one overwritten annex URL; CI-only rollover checks. All are in §05, rejected for the reasons given there.

Consequences. Adding an engine touches one more file, and CI names everything that is missing. No score, parameter or migration changes. One new preflight and CI gate, "scoring families match the manifest", is added to scripts/preflight.sh in the same change (planning rule 5a). One new scheduled check is added to the workflow catalog.

Amends. ADR-102 (the pages read their engine and season from the manifest; the annex is per season), ADR-092 (the annex address), ADR-097 (immutability becomes a check). Relates to. ADR-104, ADR-031, ADR-085.
