Documentation · look and feel · before/after · 24 Oct 2026

The handbook already wears the ADR design. In html-preview it lost its stylesheet.

Through the README’s html-preview links, every handbook page opened in plain browser defaults while the ADR pages looked right. The cause was one line in one stylesheet. With the fix, the handbook, governance and documentation pages open in the ADR design there too, and nothing changes when the files are opened directly.

E1 A, E2 A, E3 A accepted 24 Oct · the fix goes to main · E2 and E3 follow with their own before/after 33 pages + 1 stylesheet local rendering byte-identical (28/28)

§ 1 · the cause

A relative @import that html-preview cannot follow

  • The ADR design lives in doc/adr/assets/adr.css. ADR pages link it directly. The 33 handbook-family pages linked only doc/handbook/assets/handbook.css, whose first line pulled the ADR design in: @import url("../../adr/assets/adr.css").
  • html-preview.github.io loads every <link>ed stylesheet from GitHub, but it resolves an @import inside one against its own address, where the file does not exist. So those pages got only the handbook additions and no base design.
  • Measured through html-preview today: ADR pages render in Inter with the navy edge; every handbook page rendered in Times with no edge, and on a phone the ingestion page was 477 px wide.
  • Opened as files, both always looked the same, which is why it went unnoticed.

§ 2 · the fix

Each page links the ADR stylesheet itself

  • All 33 pages on handbook.css now link adr.css first, then handbook.css, and the @import is gone. The cascade order is the same as before.
  • Pages: 29 handbook pages including PAGE_TEMPLATE.html, doc/index.html, governance, rules, evidence, the archive index, and two older plans that use the handbook stylesheet.
  • Tests, seen RED first: python/tests/test_docs_stylesheets.py DOCSTYLE.01 (no stylesheet under doc/ imports another), DOCSTYLE.02 (every page on handbook.css links adr.css before it), DOCSTYLE.03 (every linked stylesheet exists).
  • The documentation standard states the rule, so new pages keep it.
  • Not touched: the generated ADR and Markdown-rendered pages (already link adr.css), plans with their own styles, mockups, the WordPress page sources and the published calculator pages.

§ 3 · no deterioration

Opened as files: byte-identical

Seven pages (the six below and ADR-115), at 1280 and 375 px, light and dark: all 28 screenshots are byte-identical before and after. The change only matters where an @import cannot be followed.
