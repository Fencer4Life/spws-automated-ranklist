Entry list · Registration · Brainstorm

A fencer changes their mind: how to withdraw from the entry list

Today nobody can take themself off „Lista zgłoszonych”. This page states what the system gives us to work with, shows three ways to add a withdrawal, and recommends one. Nothing is built; you choose, then I write the TDD plan.

Awaiting your choice 3 options · 4 questions no code written

01 · What exists today

The facts every option has to live with

  • There is no withdrawal. A fencer can add an entry and correct it („Popraw dane”), never remove it. The only removal today is an administrator deleting the row in the database by hand; I found no screen for it.
  • We hold no way to reach the fencer. The privacy notice says the e-mail is a one-time check and is not kept, and the system sends no e-mail at all. So „we will mail you a withdrawal link” is not available without a new consent text and a mail service.
  • Identity is the declared name and birth year. A fencer who returns re-enters surname, given name and birth year; that lands on their existing row and lets them correct it. Anyone who knows a fencer's name and birth year can already rewrite that fencer's weapons. This is an accepted posture (ADR-079 §4), and the birth year is not secret.
  • The registration window closes at the deadline (or the event start where no deadline is set); the entry list stays visible until the event starts.
  • No payment is tracked. The system only shows transfer details; a refund is between the fencer and the organizer.
  • The organizer's start files are built at the moment of download from the current list, so a withdrawn fencer disappears from them with no further step.

Sources: doc/handbook/reference/registration-lifecycle.html, supabase/migrations/20260828000003_registration_edit_handle.sql, frontend/src/components/RegistrationForm.svelte.

02 · The options

Three ways to withdraw

In the mocks the yellow dashed outline marks what is new. Everything else is the form as it is today. Labels are in Polish as the fencer will read them; the language switch gives the English ones.

Option A · „Wycofaj zgłoszenie” beside „Popraw dane” recommended

The fencer opens the registration form and enters their name and birth year, exactly as they do today to correct an entry. The form recognises the existing entry and the last screen gains one more button. A confirmation step, then the row is gone. The entry list gets a small link under the list that leads here, so the door is where people look for it.

Against

For
  • The shortest path: find your name, one field, done. The tiles count down at once.

Against
  • Puts a „remove” control on every row of a public list. The check is the same name and birth year as option A, but here the name is already filled in, so mischief costs one guessed number. The age category on the row narrows the guess to ten years.
  • Changes the list we finished today: rows become tappable, which also affects the phone layout and needs its own pixel sheet.
  • A wrong tap on a neighbour's row is easy on a phone.

Option C · A personal withdrawal link, given at registration

The last screen of the form shows a private link that belongs to this entry. Whoever holds the link can withdraw the entry; nobody else can. The fencer saves or copies it; opening it later shows the entry and one button.

For
  • The only option where knowing a name and birth year is not enough: real proof that the person withdrawing is the person who registered.
  • One tap to withdraw for a fencer who kept the link.
Against
  • People lose links. Without a second path, a fencer without the link is back to writing to the organizer, which is the by-hand step we want to remove.
  • The 80 people already entered for Łomianki have no link and cannot get one.
  • Needs a second, lasting key on each entry: the existing correction handle changes every time the identity is re-entered, by design, so it cannot serve as a link that must keep working.
  • Stronger than the rest of the form: the same entry could still be rewritten by name and birth year, so the link protects withdrawal only.

Looked at and set aside

  • A withdrawal link by e-mail. We keep no address and send no mail; it would need a new privacy text, a new consent version and a mail service.
  • An administrator's „remove” button only. It keeps a person in the loop for every change of mind, where the aim is that the fencer does it alone.

✓ Recommendation: option A

It is the one that works for everyone, including the people already entered, on any device, and it adds no new idea to the form: withdrawing becomes the third thing a returning fencer can do, after adding a weapon and correcting a name. Its one weakness, that a name and a birth year are enough, already exists for corrections; question Q4 makes every withdrawal visible to the administrator so a wrongful one is noticed and undone by registering again.

04 · Your decisions

Four questions, each with my recommendation

  1. Q1 · Which option? Recommended: A.
  2. Q2 · Until when can a fencer withdraw? Recommended: until the event starts, not only until the registration deadline. Most changes of mind come after the deadline, the list stays visible until the start, and the organizer's files follow the list. After the deadline a withdrawal is final, because signing up again is closed; the confirmation text will say so.
  3. Q3 · What happens to the entry? Recommended: the row is deleted. Nothing marked „withdrawn” stays on the public list, and we keep no personal data we no longer need. Before the deadline the fencer can simply register again.
  4. Q4 · Does anyone get told? Recommended: yes, a Telegram message to the administrator for each withdrawal (event, name, weapons, time), sent automatically. It costs nobody a step, and it is the answer to a withdrawal made by the wrong person. How the existing alert for identity changes is wired will be checked in the plan and reused if it fits.
