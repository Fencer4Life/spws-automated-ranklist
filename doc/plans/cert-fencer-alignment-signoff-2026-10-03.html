Safety

What protects CERT and PROD

  • PROD is read-only, and the database enforces it. Every PROD query runs inside BEGIN TRANSACTION READ ONLY. Probed on PROD today: the read works, and even a temporary table is refused.
  • PROD cannot be a target. The tool writes LOCAL or CERT only, and the recompute drain refuses any database URL but the target's.
  • One transaction that checks itself. Before it returns, the roster must equal PROD's in every column, and every person must hold exactly the rows they held before, in every referencing table. Otherwise it raises, and nothing persists.
  • A restore point. Before the apply, CERT's roster and the pairing are saved to a file that is never overwritten. --mode restore runs the inverse pairing through the same function. pgTAP ALIGN.28 proves it restores every id, value and result.
  • Tests:
    • pgTAP ALIGN.01–28: swaps and cycles of ids, every reference, aliases moving between people, refusals that change nothing, the dry run, the audit history, the restore;
    • pgTAP REG.01–08;
    • pytest PROMO.REFRESH.01–23 and PROMO.ID.01–16;
    • full pytest: 1,642 passed.
  • Four pgTAP files fail on today's LOCAL (19, 74, 80, 85). This morning's trial scored PPW1 and locked the 2026/27 season there, and these tests expect a fresh season, as CI has. Before the release, LOCAL is rebuilt from the seed and aligned again, and scripts/preflight.sh must exit 0.
