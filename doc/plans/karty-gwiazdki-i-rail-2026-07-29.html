The one real obstacle — the colour is shared medal_col() is called once, at line 278, and its result mc is used twice: as the star fill (line 287) and as the closing stop of the bar’s gradient (line 281). The bar fades from its rung colour into the medal colour.

The dossier proves the coupling — star fills and gradient stops match exactly: 146 gold · 64 silver · 86 bronze · 143 grey of each, 439 stars in total.

So editing medal_col alone repaints 439 bars as well as 439 stars. You asked for the star icon only.

Resolution — decided

Option A, with a shape change. The star slot alone is rewritten; medal_col is left untouched so the bar gradients do not move. Podium places keep their star. Fourth place and below become a green square rather than a green star.

Why a square rather than a green star 1. It does not rely on colour. Gold, silver, bronze and green differ only in hue, and bronze against green is precisely the boundary a red-green colour-blind reader cannot resolve. Shape encodes “no medal” a second time.
2. It corrects the semantics instead of recolouring them. A grey star still reads as a lesser medal; a square does not. Star = podium, square = placement — which is the distinction the rule is actually drawing, and why a bronze star on 4th place was wrong.
3. It dissolves the colour clash. With shape carrying the meaning, the fact that #12704a equals RUNG_COL["MŚW"] and WEAPON_COL["FOIL"] stops being a legibility problem. The requested green is used as-is.
Draw it as <rect>, never as a glyph .star is {{font-size:12px}} with no font-family, so it inherits the document serif stack. A ■ (U+25A0) would render at the mercy of whichever fallback font resolves — geometric glyphs vary widely in size and baseline — and its advance width differs from ★, which would shift the place number pinned at x + 13. An SVG <rect> has exact geometry, no font dependency, and leaves the 13px offset intact.

Execution path

The dossier is a generated artefact, so there are two places to change and they must not drift.

  1. Edit the dossier directly — immediate result, strict scope. A rebuild would otherwise also pull in the §06/§07 rewrite done earlier today, which is more than the two changes requested.

Mechanically, in the dossier the star change is a targeted rewrite of class="star" fill="…" only — the stop-color occurrences carrying the same hex must be left alone. That asymmetry is the whole reason this is scripted rather than done with a find-and-replace.
