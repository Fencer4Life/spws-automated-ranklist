Decide this first

What I need from you

Sign off each event by name. Each P row (PENDING, printed POL) and each R row (rejected, but the surname is an SPWS starter's), listed under its event, is either A linked to the candidate (an identity entry in the event's override file, the fencer named) or B EXCLUDED. The recommendation is in the row and applies unless you say otherwise.

→ How to answer

Shortest: “accept all”: the four events signed off, every row as recommended.

Each signed-off event is then re-staged with its override file where it has one, checked (no PENDING left, the decided rows as chosen, nothing else changed) and committed on LOCAL; then released and run on CERT and PROD, one at a time, machine-checked against LOCAL.

✓ Checked against EVF’s published results

Every row the drafts store (12, 7, 15 and 5) has EVF’s row of the same weapon, gender, category, place and surname, with the same N. No bracket here had a fencer who did not start, did not finish or withdrew during the direct elimination, so EVF and our N rule agree on all of them.

Rows added are roster fencers with a PPW or MPW result who entered the event for any federation. Rows removed are stored today but are not an exact identity of an SPWS starter; the reason is given per row.
