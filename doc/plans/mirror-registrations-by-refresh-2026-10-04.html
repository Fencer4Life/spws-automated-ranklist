LOCAL mirror · registrations · ADR-036 §3 · Record

The LOCAL mirror copies registrations like the refresh

mirror-prod-local.sh loaded PROD’s registrations with a copy of its own, which left out the club and FTL name. Promote’s LOCAL rehearsal then stopped at input.registrations. The mirror now uses the CERT refresh’s own copy and checks that the result matches PROD.

Built and verified on LOCAL, 4 Oct pytest PROMO.REFRESH.24–28 no migration Not pushed: waits on your go

01 · Why

Same rows, different hash

On 3 October the rehearsal mirrored PROD to LOCAL. Both held 90 PPW1 registrations, but promote’s gate refused on input.registrations and wrote nothing (promote-rehearsal-2026-10-03.html, step 7). The cause is in what step 5 inserted:

ColumnThe fingerprint hashes itThe refresh copied itOld mirror step 5
names, gender, birth year, weapons, fencer linkyesyesyes
txt_clubyesyesno: 41 of PPW1’s 90 and 4 of PEW5efs’s 18 have one on PROD
txt_ftl_nameyesyesno
txt_consent_versionnono (stays on PROD)copied

The fingerprint (fn_event_input_fingerprint, migration 20261003000014) hashes the columns the refresh copies, so a LOCAL copy without clubs can never match PROD.

02 · What changed

One copy, used by both

  • Step 5 of scripts/mirror-prod-local.sh is now one line: python -m python.pipeline.promotion.refresh --target local --mode registrations. The script writes no registration SQL of its own.
  • run_copy_registrations in python/pipeline/promotion/refresh.py:
    • refuses any target but LOCAL. CERT keeps copying only the ingested event, in --mode apply --event (ADR-108 §2);
    • refuses unless LOCAL’s roster equals PROD’s, id for id, because the entries carry PROD’s fencer ids;
    • sends every PROD event with entries through fn_replace_event_registrations, the same call and columns the refresh uses. No e-mail hash, edit token or consent stamp;
    • fails unless each event’s registrations part of the input fingerprint then equals PROD’s.
  • --event CODE narrows the copy to one event.
  • PROD is only read, inside a read-only transaction, as in every refresh mode.

04 · Verified on real data, 4 Oct

LOCAL’s inputs equal PROD’s

  • The copy alone, on a LOCAL rebuilt from PROD’s seed: PEW5efs 2026/27 got 18 entries and PPW1 2026/27 got 90; both registrations parts equal PROD’s.
  • The full mirror (mirror-prod-local.sh, exit 0):
    • roster equal to PROD’s, id for id;
    • step 5 reported “local registrations equal PROD’s in 2 event(s)”;
    • 373 fencers, 2 same-name pairs, 108 registrations.
  • Every part of the input fingerprint, LOCAL against PROD, after that mirror: PPW1 and PEW5efs equal as a whole, no part differing. This is the comparison promote’s gate makes.
  • Side effects: none. Today’s re-export of the seed was byte-identical to the committed seed_prod_2026-10-04.sql, and the seed pointer was restored.

06 · Not changed

Boundaries

  • The CERT refresh: it still copies only the ingested event’s registrations.
  • fn_replace_event_registrations and the fingerprint: no migration.
  • The seed: registrations stay out of it (ADR-079 keeps them ephemeral).
  • LOCAL no longer holds PROD’s consent stamps, by the same data-minimisation rule as CERT.
