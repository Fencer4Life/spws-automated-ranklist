01 · The contract

What changes, and what must not

Changes (all of them)

  1. Direction: earlier months above the focused month, later below (D1).
  2. ▲ ▼ buttons exactly as in the approved mock (D2, D3).
  3. "najbliższe zawody" follows the direction. Its arrow always points to where the opening month is drawn (§2.4). Look, label and positions are unchanged.
  4. The heavier season line lands between seasons. This follows from the direction change, with no code of its own.
  5. The calendar's title unfolds or squeezes by fit, in both languages (D5).
  6. No invisible keyboard stops, in the drum's turned-away months or in the closed drawer (D6). No pixel changes.
  7. Month labels for screen readers follow the page's language (D7). No pixel changes.
  8. Months with 7 or more events stop their preview before the arrows (D9). Nothing changes until such a month exists.
  9. Keyboard ↑/↓ (and only if you choose it, wheel or swipe) (D8).
  10. Translation tests that fail on untranslated UI text (§05).
  11. The card's left edge, only once reproduced (D4).

Unchanged, and checked by §12

  • Tiles (sizes, colours, fonts, edges, ring, registration dot, overlap and fan), seams, the event card, the caret and the footer.
  • The SPWS bar's ☰, logo and language switch; the other three pages' bars and titles; the drawer's look.
  • The opening month, skipping empty months, taps on rows and tiles, and the 0.42 s turn.
  • The drum's geometry: 246 px tall, θ 26°, R 178 px, perspective 760 px.

03 · The three remaining items, one by one

D7, D8, D9

D7 · Screen-reader month labels are English on the Polish page

Evidence. Measured on the live Polish page: 66 of 67 English labels are the drum's months ("January 2022" … "June 2027"). The row's aria-label comes from monthLabel() (calendarMonths.ts:237), which uses a hard-coded English MONTH_NAMES. The visible seam uses seamLabel(), which is localised: month_N + year. The 67th label is "Menu" on ☰ (SiteBar.svelte:11, App.svelte:33); it is correct Polish too, so it is allowlisted, not changed.

Fix. aria-label={seamLabel(row)}. A screen reader then says exactly what the eye reads: „Październik 2026" in Polish, "October 2026" in English. MonthRow.label stays, because nothing else changes. No pixel changes.

D7 Recommendation: do it as above

A richer label, such as „Październik 2026, 5 zawodów", is possible with the existing Polish plural forms. But it adds words the eye does not see, so I'd leave it out.

D8 · Keyboard ↑/↓, mouse wheel, swipe

Background. On 29 Aug you locked "taps only: no sliding, wheel, swipe or drag" for the drum. The ▲ ▼ buttons are taps, so they fit that rule. Keyboard keys don't conflict with anything. Wheel and swipe do: the drum is part of a scrolling page (ADR-084 §17), so a wheel or a swipe that starts over the drum would turn the drum instead of scrolling the page.

OptionWhat you getCost
(a) Keyboard onlyWith focus anywhere in the drum (▲, ▼, a month, a tile or the pill): ↑ steps earlier and ↓ later, exactly like the buttons. The page does not scroll for that key press.none visible
(b) + wheelOn a desktop, one wheel notch over the drum turns one month (throttled).Scrolling down the page with the pointer over the drum spins the calendar instead: a scroll trap.
(c) + swipeOn a phone, a vertical swipe on the drum turns it.The same trap, worse: phones scroll by swiping, and the drum fills a third of the screen.

D8 Your choice. Recommendation: (a)

(a) adds keyboard control with no downside. (b) and (c) reverse your 29 Aug decision and trap page scrolling. The tests for (b) and (c) are listed in §06 in case you choose them.

D9 · A month with 7 or more events reaches the buttons

Evidence. A receded month's tiles take 41 × n − 3 px. The buttons start at 252 px (github.io, 320), 264 px (WordPress, 320) and 300 px (wider screens). So a neighbouring month with 7 tiles (284 px) would run under them on a 320–360 px phone. The busiest month today is October 2026 with 5 events (PZSz PPS included).

Fix. A receded month's tile strip stops 6 px before the buttons: .up .rw, .dn .rw { max-width: min(calc(100% - 42px), 294px) }. Receded months already hide what overflows, so only the cut-off point moves. A month still shows all its events once focused. Nothing changes until a month with 7 or more events exists.

D9 Recommendation: do it as above

The alternatives:

  • Let the button cover the 7th tile's edge.
  • Fan the receded tiles like the focused row. That changes the look of every crowded preview.

09 · Drafts, for sign-off before they are written to disk

ADR-084 amendment (2026-10-06)

Amendment (2026-10-06) — time runs downward, and the drum shows how it turns

Fencers reported the calendar as not intuitive. Source: doc/plans/kalendarz-beben-strzalki-plan-2026-10-06.html; mock: doc/plans/kalendarz-beben-strzalki-mock-2026-10-06.html.

L · Time runs downward

§C's direction sentence is superseded. Earlier months sit above the focused row and later months below, so reading down the drum is reading forward in time. Rows sit at rotateX(−i·θ) translateZ(R) and the drum turns by rotateX(+active·θ); the two signs flip together and nothing else reads them.

"Future above" was never chosen: it arrived with the cylinder's angle sign on 29 Aug. Reversing it restores §2's original orientation, puts the heavier season-boundary rule between two seasons, and makes DOM and tab order run top to bottom.

M · Two floating step buttons

§6 is refined. The whole row stays the tap target, and two buttons make that visible:

  • What they do: ▲ moves to the nearest earlier month with competitions and ▼ to the nearest later one, as tapping the neighbouring row does (stepRow()).
  • Where they sit: beside the neighbouring months, right of their left-aligned tiles, at most 300 px from the left edge.
  • How they render: as siblings of the drum inside the viewport, at full opacity and never clipped.
  • At the ends: a button is removed when nothing lies further that way, and focus passes to the other.
  • Crowded months: a receded month's tiles stop 6 px before the buttons.

Measured at 320, 375 and 1,180 px, the buttons cover no tile, seam label or jump control. Buttons centred on the top and bottom edges were rejected: they covered the jump control at 320 px. Wheel, swipe and drag stay out, unless D8 says otherwise.

N · The jump control's arrow points to the opening month

§J's positions and look are unchanged; the cases they serve follow the direction. When the opening month is drawn above, the control rides the adjacent upper seam with ↑. When it is below, the control pins to the lower edge with ↓, or ← when it is the adjacent row. The pinned case no longer carries a row index.

O · What cannot be seen cannot be reached

Months two or more rows from the focus are inert: they were already faint or invisible and untappable, and keyboard and screen readers now skip them too. A row's accessible name is its seam text in the page's language, not a hard-coded English month.

P · Keyboard (if D8 (a))

With focus inside the drum, ↑ and ↓ step like the buttons and do not scroll the page.

ADR-090 amendment (2026-10-06)

Amendment (2026-10-06) — the calendar's title is its menu name; a closed drawer is inert

§5's phone title for the calendar is superseded, along with the 2026-10-03 amendment's „Kalendarz" / "Calendar".

  • The calendar's title reads its drawer entry, „Znajdź zawody" / "Competition Finder", wherever that fits.
  • It squeezes to „Zawody" / "Competitions", and in English finally to "Events", only when the room the title actually has is too small. Fit is measured, not tied to a screen width.
  • The same applies to the github.io header. Font, size and the mark's 84 px are unchanged.
  • The other three pages keep §5's 430 px switch.

The drawer is inert while closed in every mode, so its links are no longer reached by keyboard or screen reader while off-screen.

  • Docs search: Meilisearch was down because Docker was not running. Start Docker, then docker compose -f tools/docs-search/docker-compose.yml --env-file tools/docs-search/.env up -d from the primary checkout. The spws-docs MCP may still fail to connect; query http://127.0.0.1:7700/indexes/spws_docs/search with the key from tools/docs-search/.env, and never print the key.

  • Demo mode loads no calendar events (App.svelte returns early in reloadCalendar). Browser tests must stub the calendar request with page.route. The e2e harness builds the custom element and serves /index.ce.html on port 4174.
