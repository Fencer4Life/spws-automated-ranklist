2 · Decisions

D2 and D3 were decided on 29 Sep. D1 is still open. Your request for examples showed a side effect of the cap as first proposed: every capped fencer ties with the fencer who beat them. The revised question has a recommendation. Approving this plan accepts it. To choose otherwise, reject with a note, for example "D1: cap at the fencer ahead".

D1 · How should the order cap work? — open

Examples first: order-cap-examples-2026-09-29.html shows four simulated joined brackets, every fencer before and after the cap, and the distribution behind the 5.9%.

  • At least 1 point below the fencer of your category directly aheadrecommended
    • There are no ties with the fencer who beat you: 0 in the simulation, against 11,234 under option 2.
    • 6.6% of fencers are capped instead of 5.9%, with a median cut of 10.8 points.
    • No-loss still holds. The smallest step between consecutive places in any single category is exactly 1 point (the table for K = 2 and 3), so the cap never reaches below "category alone".
    • Tied places share one cap, with no step between them.
  • At most the score of the fencer ahead, as first proposed. Every capped fencer ties with them. In the largest case the winner and two fencers they beat all have 112.20.
  • No cap. 41.4% of simulated brackets then have a fencer who outscores someone of their own category who beat them, by up to 50 points. No choice of weights avoids this: at K = 8, N = 15 the weights would need at least 55.5 points and can have at most 54.7.
D2 · The released place-and-medal engine — decided 29 Sep: delete it

SPWS_PLACE_MEDAL_V1_2026_2027 is deleted, and the new engine takes its place for PPW, MPW and the season default. The delete covers its function, dispatcher branch, registry row, scoring.ts branch and SE27.ENG tests.

  • It has scored nothing in any environment (§1).
  • ADR-103 §3 is the precedent.
  • The delete is conditional. If a revision or a type row still names the engine at deploy time (the race in §10), it stays registered and unused, and nothing fails.

4 · The engine, exactly

Inputs per result, all already written by the JOINED_BRACKET_CATEGORY_PLACE module:

  • N — the whole bracket;
  • p — the joined place;
  • K — the fencer's own category in the bracket;
  • m — the place within that category, with ties 1, 2, 3, 3, 5;
  • b — fencers strictly below.

"Passed by another category" is p − m ≥ 1: exactly p − m fencers of other categories finished strictly ahead. "Single category" is K = N.

N ≤ 3                          → N − p + 1                                  method TABLE
N ≥ 16, or K = N               → EVF(N, p)                                  method EVF_CLASSIC
4 ≤ N ≤ 15 and K < N           → EVF(N, p) + a + b′ + last                  method EVF_JOINED
    a    = w[m] × ∛K     when p − m ≥ 1 and b ≥ 1, else 0     w = 44, 26, 13, 8, 4 (m ≥ 5 → 4)
    b′   = 3.5 × b       when p − m ≥ 1 and b ≥ 1, else 0
    last = 3             when b = 0, else 0
order cap (writer, per category tournament; D1 recommended form):
    score(m) = min( raw(m), score(fencer of the same category directly ahead) − 1 )
    fencers tied on the same place share one cap; without the "− 1" if D1 goes to option 2
final = ROUND( (raw − cap reduction) × type coefficient, 2 )       rounded once

EVF(N, p) = mp − (mp − 1) · ln p / ln N + de_round · rounds(N, p) + podium(p) · 3 · ∛N
            with the season's EVF settings (2026/27: mp 50, DE round 10, podium 3/2/1)
Why the cap cannot cost anyone points

The exhaustive check covers every (N, K, m, p) position of a joined bracket of 4–15, 2,375 positions in all. At every one, raw ≥ alone(K, m). The smallest margin is +0.44, at (15, 9, 8, 14). Here alone(K, m) is K − m + 1 when K ≤ 3, and EVF(K, m) otherwise.

The cap, by induction down the category. Suppose the fencer directly ahead, at category place m − 1, scores at least alone(K, m − 1). Two consecutive places in any single category of K ≤ 14 differ by at least 1 point. The smallest step is exactly 1, in the table for K = 2 and 3; EVF steps are larger (1.25 at the bottom of 15). So that fencer's score − 1 ≥ alone(K, m). The capped score is the smaller of raw(m) ≥ alone(K, m) and that value, so it is also ≥ alone(K, m).

The cap runs only in joined brackets of 4–15. From 16, EVF steps at the bottom of a large bracket drop below 1 point (0.19 between the last two of 64), so a 1-point cap there would cut single-category EVF scores.

The check is pinned as a test on raw scores, and the induction above carries it to capped scores. A seeded simulation of 20,000 brackets pins the combination (EJ27.NOLOSS.02).

6 · ADR-104 draft Status: Proposed — awaiting your sign-off

ADR-104: SPWS 2026/2027 — EVF with a joined-bracket premium replaces the place-and-medal engine

Supersedes: ADR-103 §1, the place-and-medal strategy, which is deleted before it scores a result.

Amends:

  • ADR-103:
    • §2 — PPW, MPW and the season default name the new engine;
    • §4 — the joined module pairs with the new engine, and recompute fails closed on an incomplete bracket;
    • §5 — the components and the method;
    • §7 — the pages.
  • ADR-102 — the module gains the engine and a bracket helper.
  • ADR-092 — the annex content.
  • ADR-085 — the calculator (D3).

Relates to: ADR-097 (the lock and the §11 gate), ADR-042/045 (the static dispatcher), ADR-049, ADR-036 amendment (bootstrap ordering) and ADR-083 (anon allowlist, unchanged).

Context

The place-and-medal engine released on 28 Sep (log₂N + 3.5 × b + 13/7/3 × ∛K up to 31) removes the loss from joining, but it moves away from EVF even where nothing is joined. A single-category bracket of 8 scores 53.5 for the winner, where EVF gives 98.0.

The new goal has two parts:

  • EVF parity for every single-category bracket;
  • a premium that makes joining worth it for older categories, with no fencer below what their category alone would give.

The weights were tuned for this and checked exhaustively. The evidence is in the weights page:

  • the minimum margin over alone is +0.44 across 2,375 positions;
  • without an order cap, 41.4% of simulated brackets invert within a category, by up to 50 points;
  • with the cap, 5.9% of fencers are capped;
  • 45.6% of fencers score above the same place in a single-category bracket of N;
  • the highest score seen is 233.8, against a bracket winner of 112.2.

No joined order is stored in history (3 joint slices, 8 results on LOCAL), so there is no replay of past events.

Decision

  1. New released strategy fn_score_spws_evf_joined_v1_2026_2027(n, place, k, m, below, mp, de_round, gold, silver, bronze). It is registered as SPWS_EVF_JOINED_V1_2026_2027, labelled „SPWS — EVF z premią w stawce łączonej (od sezonu 2026/2027)”, and paired with JOINED_BRACKET_CATEGORY_PLACE.
    • It returns the raw, unrounded breakdown of §4 and holds no cap.
    • The EVF part calls fn_score_evf_classic_v1_2025_2026, which keeps its own POWER(n, 1.0/3).
    • The premium uses cbrt(), as ADR-103 §1 did for the medal (SE27.ENG.14).
  2. The order cap lives in the writer. fn_calc_tournament_scores computes raw totals. For a joined bracket of 4–15, it then walks the tournament in int_category_place order. Each row is capped at 1 point below the capped score of the category place directly ahead (D1). Tied places share one cap. The reduction goes in num_cap_reduction, and the cap never runs for single-category brackets or from 16.
    • A tournament is one category of one bracket (_plan_joined_recompute already refuses to merge two).
    • Every PPW and MPW participant is stored (unmatched fencers are auto-created, FR-141), so the cap sees the whole category.
    • The strategy stays per-row and IMMUTABLE. The preview assumes one category, where the cap never binds.
  3. Assignment. For SPWS-2026-2027, the season default, PPW and MPW move to the new engine. PPS, MPS, PEW, MEW, MSW and PSW stay on EVF classic (the ADR-103 amendment stands).
    • The move is a replacement of fn_backfill_scoring_engines. It is also called from supabase/seed_post_backfill.sql, as ADR-036 requires.
    • It keeps the §11 gate: a season with a scored result is not reassigned. It raises a NOTICE that names the privileged revision.
  4. SPWS_PLACE_MEDAL_V1_2026_2027 is deleted (D2): its function, dispatcher branch, registry row, scoring.ts branch and tests. It has 0 revisions and 0 results on LOCAL, CERT and PROD (verified 29 Sep). The delete runs only when no season, type row or revision names it; otherwise it stays registered and unused.
  5. Storage.
    • The enum_score_method value PLACE_MEDAL is renamed EVF_JOINED. No row holds it.
    • num_field_pts is dropped from tbl_result, tbl_result_draft and vw_score.
    • num_last_bonus and num_cap_reduction are added, NOT NULL DEFAULT −1, with CHECK ≥ 0 or −1. The reduction is stored as a non-negative amount, because a negative component would collide with −1.
    • The components-match-method CHECK is rewritten for TABLE, EVF_CLASSIC and EVF_JOINED.
    • num_medal_bonus and num_below_pts keep their names and now hold a and b′.
  6. Module and recompute. MODULE_BY_ENGINE maps the new code to JOINED_BRACKET_CATEGORY_PLACE, with the module logic unchanged. Recompute of a joined bracket refuses when the stored rows across its category tournaments do not add up to the stored N. The message points to re-ingestion, because K = N decides "single category".
  7. Public pages.
    • scoring.ts gains the engine and scoreBracket(), which applies the same cap for the annex example and the tests.
    • The annex becomes the approved bilingual proposal, and its formula moves to the generated module block.
    • The calculator follows D3.
    • fn_public_scoring_params keeps its shape.

Alternatives considered

  • Edit the place-and-medal strategy in place. Rejected: released strategies are immutable, and its name would describe a formula it no longer is.
  • Keep it unused. Rejected in D2: it would be a dead Admin option and dead tests. ADR-103 §3 is the precedent.
  • No order cap. Rejected in D1: 41.4% of brackets invert within a category.
  • Weights large enough to avoid inversions. Impossible: K = 8, N = 15 needs at least 55.5 points but allows at most 54.7.
  • A medal for category places 1–3 only. Rejected: no-loss then fails for places 4 and below. That is why the weights run to 5th and below.
  • Plain EVF for the youngest category in the mix. Dropped on 29 Sep: it makes the table harder to read.
  • The cap inside the strategy. Impossible: it needs the sibling rows.
  • The cap as a negative component. Rejected: it collides with the −1 "not used" value.

Consequences

  • A single-category bracket scores exactly EVF at every N, and every bracket from 16 up is EVF on the joined place and N.
  • The no-loss promise covers joined brackets of 4–15 only, and the annex says so.
  • A fencer passed early in a small joined bracket can score far above the winner: 211.92 against 112.20 in the 15-fencer example. This follows from ∛K and b′. The annex proposal does not say it yet, so step 8 adds one plain sentence to its rules section.
  • Every reader of components moves to the new pair of columns: vw_score, fn_commit_event_draft, export.ts, types.ts, export_seed.py and the drilldown. No reader re-sums components; they read num_final_score.
  • If 2026/27 scores a result before the deploy, the season needs one privileged revision (ADR-097), and the old engine stays registered.

8 · Scenario walk-throughs

  1. PPW1 2026/27, a joined V1 + V2 + V3 epee bracket of 10, first on LOCAL, then CERT and PROD after the go.
    • PPW resolves to SPWS_EVF_JOINED_V1_2026_2027, whose module is JOINED_BRACKET_CATEGORY_PLACE.
    • Each fencer is filed under their own V-category tournament with the joined place, N = 10, and K, m and b.
    • The writer scores EVF_JOINED, then caps per category. For the example order, the results are the EJ27.ENG.05 values.
    • Drilldown shows EVF place points, DE bonus, podium bonus, „Premia za miejsce w kategorii”, „Za zawodników za Tobą”, and where they apply „Premia za ostatnie miejsce” and „Limit kolejności”.
    • The first score locks the season.
  2. A single-category bracket of 8, all V2. K = N, so the method is EVF_CLASSIC. The values are the EVF table (98.00 … 1.00), and a, b′, last and cap are −1.
  3. A joined bracket of 15 with 8 in V2, where V2 wins, V1 is 2nd and V2 is 3rd. The 3rd has a raw 151.52 and is capped 1 point below the V2 winner's 112.20, at 111.20, a reduction of 40.32. The V1 2nd scores 211.92: EVF 82.26 plus the premium for 1st of 7 in V1 with 13 fencers below.
  4. A joined bracket of 20. The same module applies. The score is EVF on place p of 20, with method EVF_CLASSIC. K, m and b are still written, as facts about the bracket.
  5. EVF PEW via evf-sync. PEW is on EVF classic and no joined module runs, so nothing changes.
  6. PZSz PPS1s on 3 Oct. PPS is on EVF classic, with K = N and m = place, so nothing changes.
  7. Re-ingesting a 2025/26 PPW. EVF classic with PER_CATEGORY_RENUMBER, byte-identical (EJ27.ING.02).
  8. A birth-year correction moves a V2 fencer into V3 in a fully stored joined bracket. Recompute recounts K and m over the bracket, then rescores and recaps both category tournaments. If the stored rows fell short of N, recompute refuses and names re-ingestion (EJ27.ING.04).
  9. The lock race: CERT scores a 2026/27 PEW before the deploy. The migration still adds the new engine. The backfill leaves CERT's assignment unchanged with a NOTICE, and PLACE_MEDAL stays registered there. The operator runs fn_revise_and_rescore_season on CERT with your board reference, on your go. The PEW rows rescore to identical numbers, because PEW is EVF classic.
  10. Admin opens the 2026/27 configuration. PPW and MPW show the new engine with its read-only panel, and the six others show EVF classic. After the first score, everything is disabled and the save is rejected server-side.

11 · Risks

  • The lock race is covered by §10 and scenario 9. Releasing soon shrinks the window.
  • Dropping a view column. vw_score must be dropped and recreated with its dependents. Step 4 lists them from pg_depend before writing the migration, and ./scripts/reset-dev.sh plus the full pgTAP suite prove the order.
  • A stored-row gap. If a joined bracket stores fewer rows than N, the cap sees less and the single-category test could flip on recompute. Ingestion stores every PPW and MPW participant, and recompute refuses a short bracket (EJ27.ING.04). No-loss still holds, because a cap over fewer rows is never lower.
  • Large premiums are visible. A 2nd place of 211.92 against a winner's 112.20 will draw questions. Step 8 adds a sentence saying so to the annex, and the board approves the annex before the release.
  • A change of EVF settings. The weights were checked against mp 50, DE round 10 and podium 3/2/1. The lock freezes them once the season scores. EJ27.NOLOSS.01 reads the season's settings, so an edit before the first score fails the suite rather than the promise.
