Why ten entrants show no rank

Ten of the 52 EPEE entrants carry no ranking position. Six of those are correct. Four are not, and all four are fencers who already hold results.

First, the thing that is not a problem. The seed file itself has no gaps. Every <Tireur> carries a Classement, numbered 1 to 52 with nothing missing — WYLĘGAŁA (4) is Classement="52". Nothing needs filling with 999. The dash in the comparison table is a different column: true rank, meaning “how many ranking points this fencer holds”, and a dash there means none. Those fencers are still seeded — they are simply seeded after everyone who has points, which is the fix that shipped today.

The four that are wrong

Their registrations never linked to their fencer rows, so the export had nobody to look up and treated a ranked fencer as a newcomer. ŁĘCKI Krzysztof is the clearest case: his registration and fencer #168 are byte-identical in surname, given name and birth year.

The reason is that a registration is matched once, at the moment it is created. Editing the name or year afterwards does not re-run the match. ADR-093's own notes record this entry arriving as “KRZYSZTOF Łęcki” with the fields swapped; it was corrected later, and the link was never re-derived.

Left alone, ingestion will mint a second fencer row for each of them and split their result history in two.

The decision

Which of the four should I link to their existing fencer before the files go out?

  1. ŁĘCKI only — the exact-triplet match. Unambiguous, no judgement involved.
  2. All four — also STAŃCZYK Marcin (1979 vs 1980), WALCZEWSKI Konrad (1987 vs 1990) and Spława-Neyman Maciej (1988 vs 1991, fields swapped). Each is a person the roster clearly already holds, but each needs someone to decide the birth year is a typo rather than a different person.
  3. None — accept four duplicate fencers and reconcile after the event.

Recommendation: link ŁĘCKI now, and all four if you can confirm the three birth years. ŁĘCKI carries no risk at all — the triplet matches exactly, which is the identity rule you set. The other three are the same near-miss population this subsystem was built for, and they are the ones that cost the most if left: WALCZEWSKI holds 13 results and Spława-Neyman 14, so a duplicate row does not merely mis-seed them tomorrow, it splits a real ranking history. But a birth year three years out is not something I should decide for somebody.

Separately — a date defect worth a ticket

GP8-V3-M-EPEE-2023-2024 carries dt_tournament = 2026-06-22 while its event is dated 2024-06-22. The tournament row is two years out. It changes no ranking today — the carry-over engine works from the event — but it makes ĆWIORO's and POKRZYWA's last result look 95 days old when it is over two years old, which is exactly what sent me looking for a bug that was not there.
