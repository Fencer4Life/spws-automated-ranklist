03 · Release 1

Names

1 · THE GIVEN NAME'S CAPITALS

When the fencer leaves the given-name box, and again on „Dalej”, the name is rewritten by the rule the organizer's files already use (pythonTitle, ftlSeedExport.ts:196): „aNNA” → „Anna”, „DZMITRY” → „Dzmitry”, „eszter zsuzsanna” → „Eszter Zsuzsanna”, „anna-maria” → „Anna-Maria”. The surname keeps today's live capitals.

The database applies the same rule to a newcomer's entry, whatever wrote it, so an old cached page cannot store „JOHN”. A linked entry keeps the roster's spelling. The 11 existing newcomers' names are corrected once by the migration.

2 · A TITLE WITH THE NAME

typed: Nazwisko „dr kovács”, Imię „péter”

Nazwisko

Dr. KOVÁCS

Imię

Péter

on the list: Dr. KOVÁCS Péter, placed under K

  • Accepted from any country. A short fixed list: dr, ddr, prof, ing, mag, mgr, dipl-ing, doc and their combinations, with or without the dot, at the start or the end of either box. PROD has both forms today: „DR SURNAME” and „Surname Dr.”.
  • Written one way, in front of the surname: „Dr.”, „Prof. Dr.”. The surname stays in capitals.
  • Not part of the surname for anything that compares names: the list's alphabetical order, the name search, the match with a known fencer and the reversed-fields question.
  • A word that only begins like a title („DRAGAN”, „INGRID”, „MAGDA”) is left alone: a title is a whole word.
  • Stored in the surname field, with one shared rule that splits it off wherever names are compared. No new column.
  • The organizer's files and linked entries: decision D4.
3 · THE REVERSED-FIELDS QUESTION

typed: Nazwisko „ANNALISA”, Imię „Avancini”

Czy pola nie są zamienione?

  • Asked when the surname box holds a known given name and the given-name box does not. Asked once, before identity matching; either answer is believed; after a swap, matching runs on the corrected pair.
  • The known given names come from two sources: our fencer table, read live, and the given names of the EVF ranking, kept in one small table.
  • Measured: on PROD it asks 4 entries, all truly reversed, and no other. On the EVF ranking's 2,680 fencers, 69 have a surname that is also a given name („MARTIN”, „ROMAN”) and none is asked, because their given names are known.
  • Its limit: when neither word is on a list („FRED” + „ARNOLD”), nothing is asked. The read-back below is for that.
4 · THE KNOWN GIVEN NAMES

  • 1,451 known names in all. The EVF names were read on 21 Nov through the project's own EVF client: 6 weapon-and-gender lists × 4 categories (the EVF ranking has no V0 list). The list is in the appendix.
  • Only given names are kept. No surname, country, birth date or ranking place enters the table or the repository. Left out: 3 entries that are only initials, and 4 strings that occur in the EVF data more often as a surname.
  • The monthly job (given-names-sync.yml, tool python/tools/extract_evf_given_names.py): 24 requests one second apart; it adds names it has not seen on CERT and PROD and never removes one; it reports the number added on Telegram and alerts if the EVF site refuses it. A new EVF name appears only when a new fencer first scores, so monthly is ample.
  • Comparison ignores capitals and accents („Jose” is known through „José”). A double name is known when the whole is listed or each part is.
  • Visitors cannot read the table or the roster. The form asks one function, fn_registration_name_check(surname, given name), which returns two yes/no answers.
