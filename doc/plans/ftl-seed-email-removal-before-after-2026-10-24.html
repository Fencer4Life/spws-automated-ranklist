§ 7 · the lint fixes ship nothing

Both bundles are byte-identical after the warning fixes

The svelte-check warnings were fixed by declaring in svelte.config.js the customElement option both builds already use, and by marking three deliberate one-time attribute reads, including one in the protected FtlExportElement.svelte. To prove none of that reaches what ships, I built the app and the custom-element bundle before and after: all 21 output files are byte-identical. The same holds after the Vite size-budget and type-reference changes.

What the phone fix (§ 6) changes in the bundles, and nothing else

The custom-element bundle main.ce.js is the file the organizer download page loads, and it also carries the whole admin app: the ranklist and calendar elements embed App.svelte. So the § 6 phone rules ship in it too. They are 779 characters of CSS, scoped to the event list’s own class and applied only below 600 px. They cannot reach the download form, which has its own scoped classes.

Proof, rebuilt from the final tree on 24 Oct: I took out only that 779-character block and built both bundles again. Both came out byte-identical to the before-build, all files. The source file was then restored and compared byte for byte with its backup. So the phone block is the only difference in what ships, in both bundles.

§ 5 · limits of this sheet

What it does not show

  • No dark-mode captures. The plan said “light and dark”. That was wrong for these screens. The only dark-mode rule in the frontend is a card shadow on the public calendar (EventCard.svelte). The admin form, the admin list and the download page have no dark theme, so a dark capture would equal the light one.
  • The phone overflow is fixed in § 6, a separate, intended change with its own before/after.
