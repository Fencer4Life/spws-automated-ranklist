02 · What the proposed drum does

Two changes, and one that follows from them

BehaviourTodayProposed
Where later months sitAbove the focused monthBelow it. Reading down the drum is reading forward in time, as in a list or a printed calendar.
Going forward one monthTap the upper month; the drum turns downTap the lower month, or ▼; the drum turns up, like scrolling a page down
Step arrowsNone. The only cue is the half-visible neighbouring months.▲ and ▼ float over the drum at full strength, always visible. Each does exactly what tapping that neighbouring month does, and empty months are skipped. An arrow disappears when there is no earlier or later competition.
"najbliższe zawody" (the way back)Rides the upper month when you have gone into the past; pins to the bottom edge when you have gone into the futureMirrored. It rides the upper month when you have gone into the future (the way back is up). It pins to the bottom edge when you have gone into the past. Same look, same two positions.
Keyboard and screen readersThe order of months in the page runs bottom-to-top on screenTop-to-bottom, which matches the screen. The arrows are real buttons with labels: "Poprzedni miesiąc" / "Następny miesiąc".

Why reversing is safe

The drum's direction is set by the sign of two angles, nowhere else. Skipping empty months, the opening month and the event card all work in time, not in screen direction, so they do not change. Reversing also restores the order the drum had before it became a cylinder on 29 Aug. Nobody chose "future above"; it came in with the sign of the cylinder's angle.

03 · Decide this first

Three questions, each with a recommendation

D1 Is this what "reverse the rotation" means?

The drum only turns when you tap, so "reversing the rotation" can only mean swapping which side the future is on. Turning the other way on the same tap would push the month you tapped off screen.

Recommendation: yes, past above and future below, as on the right-hand phone.

D2 Where should the arrows float? (toggle "Arrows" above)

  • A · beside the tiles: on the right of the neighbouring months, where those rows are always empty because tiles start at the left. They cover nothing. On a desktop they stay next to the tiles instead of 900 px away at the far edge.
  • B · centred on the top and bottom edges: the classic look, but they sit on the month line above and on the city line of the month below. On a desktop they drift to the middle of a 1,176 px drum.

Measured in this mock at 320, 375 and 1,180 px, both at the opening month and with the "najbliższe zawody" pill showing at the top and at the bottom:

  • A overlaps nothing at any of the three widths: no tile, no month label, no pill. The ▼ sits 9 px above the bottom pill.
  • B overlaps the "najbliższe zawody" pill at 320 px, both when it is at the top and when it is at the bottom. On desktop the arrows sit at x ≈ 558, more than 400 px from the tiles.

Recommendation: A. It is the only placement that covers nothing at every width, and it keeps the arrows next to the months they move.

D3 At the first or last competition, hide the arrow or grey it out?

Recommendation: hide it. A greyed-out button is still a target someone will tap. A missing ▼ already says "nothing later". This applies only at the very ends of the history (January 2022 and June 2027 in this pool).
