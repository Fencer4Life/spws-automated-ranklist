Rules

What changes

  • Order — both tables, the phone cards and both bar charts: Punkty descending. Equal points: the newer result first. The old ordering (current season first, then by date; MPW last in the chart) goes.
  • Counted: drawn as today, with ★ after the points and on the bar — MPW too (it had ✓).
  • Not counted: the table row (and the phone card) gets a grey background and grey text; the bar is faded; no ★, no label.
  • Carried over: keeps exactly today’s look — grey text, the ↩ season badge, the striped bar — and is simply sorted with the rest, with ★ when it counts. The grey background is what marks “not counted”, so the two never look alike.
  • Legend: „★ wliczany” replaces „★ Best” and „✓ zawsze wliczane”; the ↩ entry reads „↩ Wynik z poprzedniego sezonu” (was „↩ Przeniesione”).
  • The yellow banner „↩ Część wyników przeniesiona z poprzedniego sezonu” is removed; the ↩ on each row and bar, explained in the legend, says it instead.
  • PPW view: the same, for the domestic table and the SPWS bars (the EVF+ part stays hidden there, as today).
  • Look of the tables: each table — Zawody krajowe and Zawody EVF+, and the card list on a phone — sits in a framed panel with a light blue-grey background (#f3f6fb), a rounded border and a soft two-layer shadow, so it lifts off the white modal. Greyed rows stay clearly grey against it.

✓ Greyed means the ranking does not count it

Which results count comes from the counting the modal already does for its totals. Checked on LOCAL: for all 228 ranked fencers in the 30 sub-rankings of 2026/27 it gives the same domestic, EVF+ and PPW totals as fn_ranking_full and fn_ranking_ppw — 0 disagreements.
