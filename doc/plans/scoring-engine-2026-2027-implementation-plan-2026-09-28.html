3 · ADR-103 draft Status: Proposed — awaiting your sign-off

ADR-103: SPWS 2026/2027 — place-and-medal engine per tournament type, joined brackets scored whole, ranking entered through PPW/MPW

Amends: ADR-097 (engine per type instead of per season), ADR-098 (entry_types), ADR-102 (module and parameters; ADR-102 is itself still a Draft), ADR-092 (annex content, 64 × 64), ADR-085 (calculator joined mode and toggle), ADR-049 (renumbering becomes a named module), ADR-069 (count gate under joined N) and ADR-100 (PPS/MPS scored by the new engine). Partly supersedes: ADR-024 for types on the new engine. Relates to: ADR-042/045 (dispatcher pattern), ADR-056 (category from birth year), ADR-066 (minimum field), ADR-083 (anon allowlist), ADR-036 amendment (bootstrap ordering).

Context

The board redefines §8 for 2026/27: a joined bracket is scored by place in the whole bracket, not split. On the engine assigned in September (SPWS_FIELD_SCALED_V1_2026_2027), scoring the whole bracket makes 42–47% of joined configurations lose points, so older categories have no reason to join.

The signed-off formula scores only quantities a joining category cannot reduce: the field, the fencers with a worse result, and a medal within one's own category. Up to 31 fencers it has no loser. From 32 the board prefers the EVF algorithm and accepts the drop at the boundary, which the rules state in words.

The formula needs four inputs (N, p, K, m) where today's strategy contract passes two. It must not reach international results, where EVF publishes per category. The board also limits ranking entry to PPW and MPW.

Decision

  1. New released strategy fn_score_spws_place_medal_v1_2026_2027(n, place, k, m, …), registered as SPWS_PLACE_MEDAL_V1_2026_2027 (label „SPWS — miejsce w stawce i premia medalowa (od sezonu 2026/2027)”). It has three ranges:
    • N ≤ 3 → N − p + 1;
    • 4–31 → log₂N + 3.5 × b + 13/7/3 × ∛K when m ≤ 3 and K > m, where b is the number of fencers strictly below;
    • N ≥ 32 → fn_score_evf_classic_v1_2025_2026 with the season's EVF settings.
    Its constants are part of the version.
  2. Engine per type. tbl_scoring_type_config gains the engine. fn_resolve_scoring_params resolves it from the tournament's type. The dispatcher stays a static CASE … ELSE RAISE and gains K and m, which EVF classic ignores. For 2026/27: PPW, MPW, PPS and MPS use the new engine; PEW, MEW, MSW and PSW use EVF classic. Older seasons put EVF classic on every type, which is their current scoring.
  3. SPWS_FIELD_SCALED_V1_2026_2027 is deleted: its function, dispatcher branch, registry row, scoring.ts branch and tests. It has 0 revisions and 0 scored results on CERT and PROD (verified 27 Sep 2026). fn_backfill_scoring_engines is replaced in the same migration because it names the code.
  4. Joined-bracket modules paired with engines. python/pipeline/joined_brackets/ holds PER_CATEGORY_RENUMBER (today's _rerank_places, moved unchanged) and JOINED_BRACKET_CATEGORY_PLACE. A registry maps each engine code to exactly one module. Ingestion and RECOMPUTE_DOMESTIC use the module of the engine assigned to the type. The registry table stays metadata only; it gains a display column naming the module.
  5. Storage. tbl_result gains:
    • int_category_count (K) and int_category_place (m);
    • num_field_pts, num_below_pts and num_medal_bonus;
    • enum_score_method: TABLE, PLACE_MEDAL or EVF_CLASSIC.
    Unused values are −1, enforced by CHECK, and existing history is backfilled with −1 and EVF_CLASSIC. Ingestion writes K and m; re-ingest and recompute overwrite them.
  6. Ranking entry. The Season Scoring Rules gain entry_types. From 2026/27 it is ["PPW","MPW"], and fn_ranking_full_* admits only fencers with a result of those types in the ranking's window, in any weapon. Seasons without the key are unchanged.
  7. Public pages. scoring.ts implements the new engine (four inputs) and EVF classic. fn_public_scoring_params reports the engine and coefficient per type, and both anon allowlists change together. The annex becomes the 64 × 64 table. The calculator gets the joined mode, and its toggle compares SPWS with EVF classic.

Alternatives considered

  • Rewrite the field-scaled strategy in place. Rejected: released strategies are immutable, and the name would describe a formula it no longer is.
  • One engine for every type. Rejected: EVF points would rise by 25–32%, and small EVF categories would fall to 1–3 points.
  • Derive K and m from stored rows. Rejected: they would be wrong whenever a category fencer is excluded, and explicit columns make drilldown and audit exact.
  • NULL or NaN for "not used". Rejected. You asked for a value that says "not used". NaN also sorts above every number in Postgres and serialises as the string "NaN".
  • A cap of 31, or a taper, instead of the EVF switch. The board chose the EVF switch at 32 and accepted a documented drop at the boundary.
  • A second policy dropdown next to the engine. Rejected: the engine and its joined-bracket module are one choice.

Consequences

  • The promise that joining costs nobody points holds only for brackets of 4–31. Crossing 31 → 32 costs existing fencers 3.5–44.9 points, and §8 ust. 8 says so.
  • Fencers with only EVF, FIE or PZSz results leave the 2026/27 ranking. Earlier seasons keep their published rankings.
  • Every reader of score components handles −1 and labels components by method: fn_fencer_scores_rolling*, the vw_score* views, export.ts, export_seed.py and build_scorecards.py.
  • The anon surface changes shape but not size. The pgTAP total and Appendix D move.
  • If CERT scores a 2026/27 result before this lands, CERT needs one privileged revision (ADR-097) to reach the new assignment.

5 · Scenario walk-throughs

  1. PPW1 2026/27, a joined V1+V2+V3 epee bracket of 10, ingested on LOCAL then CERT.
    • The tournament type PPW resolves to the new engine, whose module is JOINED_BRACKET_CATEGORY_PLACE.
    • Each fencer is filed under their own V-cat tournament, keeping the joined place, and every sibling stores N = 10.
    • K and m are written per row. fn_calc_tournament_scores scores PLACE_MEDAL, and the EVF columns are −1.
    • The ranking by category shows the example's points, and drilldown shows „3/10 · 2. w kat. z 4”.
    • The first score locks the season.
  2. A joined bracket of 34. The same module applies. The engine takes the N ≥ 32 range and scores EVF_CLASSIC. The new component columns are −1, and K and m are still written, because they are facts about the bracket.
  3. EVF PEW1f. evf_sync stores EVF's own place and field, as today. PEW resolves to EVF classic, and no joined-bracket module runs. K, m and the new components are −1, and the method is EVF_CLASSIC.
  4. PZSz PPS1s, a field of 107. CommitPzszSenior keeps the original place and N = 107, and writes K = 107 and m = place. PPS resolves to the new engine; since N ≥ 32 the result is EVF classic × 1.1. The ranking counts it only for fencers who entered through PPW or MPW.
  5. Re-ingesting a 2025/26 PPW event. The type row there says EVF classic, whose module is PER_CATEGORY_RENUMBER. Output is byte-identical to today, pinned by SE27.ING.02.
  6. An EVF-only Polish fencer. They appear in the 2025/26 full ranking, as today, but not in 2026/27 until their first PPW or MPW start.
  7. Admin opens 2026/27 configuration. Before the lock: PPW/MPW/PPS/MPS show the new engine with its read-only panel, and PEW/MEW/MSW/PSW show EVF classic with its editable parameters; coefficients are editable. After the first score, everything is disabled and the save is rejected server-side.
  8. CERT scores PEW1f before the migration lands. The migration's assignment step finds a scored 2026/27 and refuses a silent switch (§11 gate). The operator runs fn_revise_and_rescore_season on CERT with a board reference. PROD is unaffected, because nothing is promoted until the deploy.
