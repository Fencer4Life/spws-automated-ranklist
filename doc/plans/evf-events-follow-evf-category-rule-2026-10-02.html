EVF events take EVF's category rule: no minimum of 5

EVF scores every category of its events, however small: one fencer, two, three. SPWS's season configuration sets a minimum of 5 for EVF-ranked types (PEW, MEW, MSW), so ingestion drops what EVF scores. Per your decision, the fix is the season configuration: the EVF minimum becomes 1 in every season, through the audited revision procedure for the locked seasons. Then 8 Polish results come back: 6 at Jabłonna 2026 and 2 at Jabłonna 2025, at exactly EVF's points.

Three decisions · answered N A, R A, ADR A

N The default for a new season

Today a new season gets an EVF minimum of 5: the column default and the season wizard both say 5. Setting the four existing seasons to 1 leaves the next season at 5 unless someone changes it.

  • A change the default to 1, in the column and the wizard. The administrator can still set another value per season.
  • B keep 5 as the default and set each new season by hand.

Recommendation: A. The rule is that EVF events follow EVF, which has no minimum; a default of 5 would quietly bring the old rule back each season.

✓ Our EVF engine is EVF's formula

All 87 Jabłonna 2026 results committed on LOCAL today carry exactly EVF's points and EVF's N. The values above are what our engine gives at the category's own N and place: the scoring needs no change, only the admission.

EVF also marks some rows “ranked: N”. The flag occurs inside these small categories and outside them, among Poles too (ATANASSOW, 2nd in V2 men's épée), so it is not a size rule. It is about EVF's own ranking and is not used here.

What SPWS does today

The minimum of 5 is SPWS's own setting

  • Each season's scoring configuration has two minimums (ADR-066): domestic int_min_participants_ppw = 1 (PPW, MPW, PSW) and EVF int_min_participants_evf = 5 (PEW, MEW, MSW). It is the same in all four seasons, LOCAL, CERT and PROD.
  • The minimum gates ingestion, not scoring: a bracket below it is skipped before any row is written. Changing it rewrites nothing already stored.
  • The EVF minimum is a locked field once a season has scored (ADR-097). The only way to change it then is fn_revise_and_rescore_season, which records the actor, the reason and a new revision, and rescores the season.
  • The scoring itself already matches EVF in every season, 2026/27 included: PEW, MEW and MSW use the plain EVF engine; the joined-bracket rules (ADR-104) apply only to PPW and MPW.

✓ The change moves no existing points

Rolled back on LOCAL: the EVF minimum set to 1 in all four seasons and all 766 scored tournaments rescored. 0 of 2,741 results change points. The minimum is not a scoring input.

! Found: the revision procedure cannot run on these seasons

fn_revise_and_rescore_season rescores every tournament of the season, including empty ones, and stops at the first without a participant count (“Tournament 408 has no participant count”). 2023/24, 2024/25 and 2025/26 hold such empty tournaments in every environment (18 on CERT and on PROD, none with results). So no locked season can be revised today. Fixed test-first in step 1: the rescore covers the tournaments that have results.

Draft · signed off with this plan

The amendment to ADR-066

Amendment 2026-10-02 · EVF events follow EVF's category rule

EVF ranks every category of its events, however small, a single fencer included, and the association scores an EVF event by EVF's rules. The EVF minimum (int_min_participants_evf, PEW, MEW and MSW) is therefore 1 in every season. The seasons that had scored were revised through ADR-097's procedure on 2026-10-02, and 1 is the default for a new season. The domestic minimum is unchanged at 1. The gate remains: a bracket with no fencer is still skipped, and the value stays a per-season setting the administrator can change.

Also recorded: ADR-097 gains a note that its revision rescores only tournaments with results; ADR-105's consequences list this as a repair-batch decision; spec Appendix C updates the ADR-066 and ADR-097 entries.
