04 · G65 · the design

A pool-only round is known by its content, never by its name

What a results page holds. FTL’s results page of a listing (/events/results/{uuid}) lists the listing’s rounds as links: a pool round as /pools/scores/{uuid}/{round}, a direct-elimination round as /tableaus/scores/{uuid}/{round}. The three stored pages in python/tests/fixtures/ftl/ each carry one pool link and one tableau link. A page with no /tableaus/ round has no DE bouts: it is a pools-only round. That is the whole signal; the listing’s name plays no part.

What exists today.

  • The domestic route (SPWS, PZSz) already fetches every listing’s results page and drops a listing without a tableau (ingest_cli._ftl_has_direct_elimination, python/pipeline/ingest_cli.py:611–620, used at :931 and :1345; ADR-057, ADR-067, ADR-076). Its test is a word search for “tableau” anywhere in the page, which today hits only that link.
  • The international route decides by name. A listing with pool words (“runda grupowa”, “pool round”, “poule”) is a pool round only when its weapon and gender also have category brackets (_drop_covered_pool_rounds, python/tools/scrape_ftl_event_urls.py:286–301, INTL.SCHED.03). A name the organiser spells differently next time is read as a bracket. A bracket listed on two days behind one link is joined into one and is final only when both rows say Finished (:227–232, INTL.SCHED.04). At Plovdiv the three day-1 rows never say Finished, so those brackets never count as final.

The change: one structural check for both routes, and the last-day rule.

#RuleWhere
R1One link listed on several days is one bracket. It is final when the row of its last listed day says Finished: that day holds the DE. Its day is that last day. The earlier rows are the pool days and decide nothing.parse_event_schedule, the twin branch at :227. Test INTL.SCHED.04 rewritten (python/tests/test_international_intake.py:565): day 1 not finished, day 2 finished ⇒ final; day 2 not finished ⇒ not final.
R2Every listing the international route is about to read is checked by its results page before its data is fetched: the page’s round links are parsed; no /tableaus/ round ⇒ a pools-only round, skipped with the reason “pool round (no direct elimination round)” and never holding the event open; a tableau round ⇒ a bracket, read on as today. The name-based pool rule is retired on this route. A listing without a results page at all is not final (unchanged).One function, has_direct_elimination(html) in python/scrapers/ftl.py, that parses the round links; the domestic route calls it in place of its word search (same answer on every stored page), the international route calls it in international_sources._other_source for each listing. One request per listing, counted by the ledger. This is also C1(2) of the combined-bracket plan, done by content.

Cost. One more request per listing on every international ingest: an event with L listings costs 1 + 2L instead of 1 + L (IMSW: 49 instead of 25). The hourly compare still reads the schedule only (ADR-120 S3), so the page check runs only when something changed. Inside the cap for any one event; two large FTL events ingesting on the same day would be close to it, and the ledger says so.

Before the code, two reads (2 requests, inside the cap, after you sign): Plovdiv’s schedule, for the exact day-1 and day-2 rows; and one known pools-only results page (Jabłonna 2025’s “runda grupowa” listing), so the pools-only fixture is a real page and the test is red for a real reason.

Documentation in the same change: ADR-105’s schedule lines (INTL.SCHED.03 becomes the content rule, INTL.SCHED.04 “final on its last day”), with ADR-076 amended to point at the shared check; the handbook’s ingestion page (present tense); RTM rows; the combined-bracket plan, rev 3, with C1(2) marked done. Tests, red first: INTL.SCHED.03 and .04 rewritten; FTL.DE.01–03 for the shared check (a page with a tableau, a pools-only page, a page with no rounds); INTL.SCHED.06 for the skip reason and that it never holds the event open.

Not in this change: C1(1) and C3 of the combined-bracket plan (sub-events and a lone guest’s DE) stay yours to decide; no re-ingest of any closed event beyond G67 and the Plovdiv proof read.
