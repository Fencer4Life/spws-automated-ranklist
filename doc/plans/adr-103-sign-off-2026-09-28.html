What ADR-103 decides, in one screen

  1. A new released strategy, SPWS_PLACE_MEDAL_V1_2026_2027, with three ranges by whole-bracket N: N ≤ 3 → N − p + 1; 4–31 → log₂N + 3.5 × fencers strictly below + medal 13/7/3 × ∛K (m ≤ 3, K > m); N ≥ 32 → EVF classic, no category medal.

  3. SPWS_FIELD_SCALED_V1_2026_2027 is deleted (0 revisions, 0 results on CERT and PROD).
  4. Joined brackets are handled by two named modules, one per engine: PER_CATEGORY_RENUMBER (today's behaviour, moved unchanged) and JOINED_BRACKET_CATEGORY_PLACE.
  5. tbl_result gains K, m, the three score components and the scoring method; "not used" is stored as −1, never NULL or NaN.
  6. From 2026/27, a fencer enters the ranking only through a PPW or MPW start in the ranking's window.
  7. The annex and calculator are rebuilt around the new engine at release.
