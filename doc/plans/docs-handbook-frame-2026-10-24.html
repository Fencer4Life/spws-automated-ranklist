§ 1 · E2, what changes

The facts card and the contents column, generated from the page itself

  • Facts card beside the title, the same card as on an ADR: Status, Owner, Type, Last verified and Page id, read from the page’s <meta> tags. “Current” is green and the template’s “Draft” is amber, using the same colours as ADR statuses.
  • Contents column on the left (“On this page”), the ADR .toc: one entry per <section id>, titled by its heading, in page order. Sticky on desktop. On a phone it sits under the title, in two columns, as on ADRs.
  • Generated, never hand-edited. scripts/render_handbook_frame.py writes both, like render_adrs.py writes ADR pages. --check runs in preflight and in CI, so a page whose sections or metadata change without a re-render fails the build.
  • Pages: the 26 handbook pages with page metadata, including PAGE_TEMPLATE.html, so new pages start with the frame. Pages without handbook metadata are left as they are: governance, rules, evidence, doc/index.html, the archive index and two older plans.
  • Tests, seen RED first: python/tests/test_render_handbook_frame.py HBFRAME.01–07: card from metadata; contents in section order; rendering twice changes nothing; the stale chip goes and other chips stay; page text untouched; every page current; preflight and CI run the check.
  • Docs: the documentation standard now states the frame rule, the validation list includes the new check, and so do doc/claude/testing.md and the release skill.

§ 2 · E2, no deterioration

Measured on all 26 pages

  • Page text unchanged: the <main> markup is byte-identical to main on all 26 pages. Reversing the three generated parts gives back each page exactly, apart from the dropped chip. In the browser, the text of <main> is identical in 54 of 56 captures. The other two are the documentation standard, where I added the paragraph that describes the frame.
  • No sideways scrolling at 1280 or 375 px: 56 of 56 captures, light and dark. Through html-preview, all six pages below load the ADR stylesheet: Inter, with the navy edge on desktop. The phone layout has no edge by design.
  • The text column is narrower: 818 px instead of 1092 px on desktop, because the contents column takes the left side. ADR pages have the same column width. Text keeps its 72-character measure. Tables and screenshots get narrower: the first registration screenshot goes from 1092 to 818 px wide and is still readable (§ 4). Page length changes by −4% to +27% on desktop (median +8%). Registration lifecycle gets shorter because its screenshots shrink. On a phone the change is +3% to +49% (median +7%), from the card and the contents list stacked above the text. The nearly empty template is the extreme.

§ 3 · E2, the one visible text change

The “Verified …” chip goes; the card shows last-verified

  • 21 pages, and the template, had a hand-written chip such as Verified 3 October 2026 under the title. It is removed, and an empty chip row goes with it. Other chips stay.
  • The card shows the page’s last-verified metadata instead. That is the value check_docs.py validates and the documentation contract maintains. The chips were never checked and had drifted.
  • What you will see: most cards say 24 October 2026. That date was set by the ownership review in today’s FTL e-mail release, when pages whose watched sources changed were reviewed and re-dated. The old chip dates were older, for example 3 October on Ingestion and 23 July on Security posture. Three pages keep earlier dates: competition and ranking 18 October, scoring and ranking 18 October, identity and recompute 2 October.

§ 5 · E3, the Decision band on a phone

The band pulls out to the page edge, not past it

  • Below 820 px the phone page margin is 20 px, but the ADR “Decision” band pulled out 24 px on each side. Through html-preview on main, ADR-115 on a 375 px phone measures 379 px wide, with the band running from −4 to 379. With the fix it is 375 px and the band runs from 0 to 375.
  • Fix: one rule in doc/adr/assets/adr.css, inside the phone media query: .section-card.decision { margin-inline: -20px; padding-inline: 20px; }. Desktop is unchanged: full-page desktop screenshots of ADR-043, ADR-080 and ADR-115, light and dark, are byte-identical (6 of 6).
  • Test, seen RED first: python/tests/test_docs_stylesheets.py DOCSTYLE.04. On phones, the band may pull out no further than the page margin.

The two crops look the same because the difference is 4 px. On main the crop is wider than the phone, which is the sideways scroll this fixes.
