Rewriting the scoring table in Polish and English

The annex still describes the place-and-medal engine, which is being deleted. This plan rewrites the whole page for the rule locked on 30 September. That rule covers the meeting, EVF, the joined-bracket premium with the 1-point cap, the coefficient and the joining rules. Polish is the default and English the twin. The page's working parts are reused.

The plan in six lines
  1. Rewrite the source doc/tools/Tabela-punktacji-SPWS_2026-2027.html. The generator copies it byte for byte to frontend/public/tabela-punktacji.html.
  2. Everything about the place-and-medal engine goes: the three-part table, K, the medal table and icons, and the N = 10 example.
  3. Four new parts: the joining rules, the joined-bracket rule, a premium table for 4–15 fencers, and a step-by-step simulator of a joined bracket. The simulator is the only part that can show the 1-point cap, because the cap depends on the whole finishing order.
  4. These parts are reused: the language switch, the season-parameter loader, the coefficient table, the calculator frame, the 64 × 64 table, locate, the CSV export and the startup-failure banner.
  5. frontend/src/lib/scoring.ts gains the new engine, test-first. The expected values come from the spec's reference implementation.
  6. The rewritten page shows numbers only once the database assigns the new engine to PPW (ADR-102). It therefore ships with the engine in the ADR-104 release, not before. Until then you review a copy in doc/plans/ with the season parameters filled in.

1 · Starting point

The locked rule, in full in doc/plans/joined-scoring-final-spec-2026-09-30.html:

  • A bracket of 1–3 fencers is a meeting and scores 3 / 2 / 1.
  • A single-category bracket of 4 or more, and any bracket of 16 or more, scores plain EVF.
  • In a joined bracket of 4–15:
    • the youngest category scores EVF;
    • an older category scores max(EVF × (1 + 0.05 · d), EVF + d);
    • nobody scores more than the fencer directly ahead, minus 1 point.
  • The result is then multiplied by the rank coefficient: PPW 1.0, MPW 1.2.
  • The joining rules are in Part B §2 of the spec.

3 · The interactive parts

Calculator (section 7)

  • Inputs kept: N (1–300), place, rank slider and type chips.
  • Joined toggle: "Twoja kategoria" (V0–V4) and "Najmłodsza kategoria w stawce" (not older than yours), with d shown between them.
  • Breakdown by case:
    • meeting: N − place + 1;
    • EVF: points for the place, DE rounds, podium;
    • joined 4–15 with d > 0: "EVF × (1 + 0,05 · d)", "EVF + d", then "the larger of the two";
    • joined 16+: "no premium from 16 fencers".
  • Result: multiplied by the coefficient.
  • The cap: one fixed row reads "Ograniczenie: najwyżej wynik zawodnika bezpośrednio przed Tobą minus 1 pkt. Zależy od całej listy; pokazuje je symulator poniżej." A link goes to section 10 (question Q1).

Full table (section 9)

  • Rows 1–3: the meeting.
  • Rows 4–64: EVF.
  • The 4–15 band note: "w stawce łączonej starsze kategorie dostają premię — tabela premii i symulator".

Simulator (section 10)

  • Building the order: buttons V0–V4 add the next fencer to the finishing order. "Cofnij" removes the last one, "Wyczyść" clears the order.
  • Status line: it shows N, the youngest category, and which rule applies: meeting, EVF, joined 4–15, or 16+.
  • Columns: place, category, d, EVF, × (1 + 0.05 · d), + d, the larger value, the cap (score ahead − 1), the result × the coefficient, the category alone, and the difference.
  • The "alone" column: a category of 1–3 alone is a meeting; a category of 4 or more is EVF for its place within the category.
  • Joining is not checked: the simulator does not verify that the line-up would be joined this way. A note says that §2 decides that. Joining stays implemented only in ingestion, in Python.

Presets. The line-ups are real and the orders illustrative.

  • A. GP2-2023-2024 men's foil, V3 3 + V4 3: the 5% at the top, + d at the bottom.
  • B. GP7-2023-2024 men's sabre, V2 12 + V3 2: the cap applies twice at the bottom, at 12th and 14th place (4.48 and 1.38).
  • C. MPW-2023-2024 women's sabre, V0 3 + V1 3 + V2 2 + V3 1: four categories, the cap applies, and the preset sets the slider to MPW 1.2.
  • D. PPW3-2025-2026 men's sabre, 17 fencers: plain EVF, no premium.

5 · Why the page cannot go live on its own

  • The annex refuses to compute with the wrong engine. It computes only with the engine assigned to PPW, and refuses any other (loadScoringParams, source line 783). After the rewrite it expects the new engine code.
  • The database still assigns the old engine. CERT and PROD assign SPWS_PLACE_MEDAL_V1_2026_2027 until the ADR-104 migration lands. Published alone, the annex would show "parameters unavailable" to the public, and a push to main deploys PROD.
  • So the page ships with the engine. It is committed only when you ask, and it goes out in the ADR-104 release, together with:
    • the SQL engine;
    • the calculator page;
    • the documentation gate.
  • The season lock (ADR-097) still applies. No 2026/27 PPW or MPW result may be scored before that release. A read-only check at the release confirms it.

10 · Questions

Q1 · How is the 1-point cap shown?
  1. A per-place calculator without the cap, plus the simulator. recommended The calculator stays simple, and the simulator shows the cap on a real order.
  2. The calculator gets an extra field: "points of the fencer directly ahead". The cap works on scores before the coefficient, so at MPW the gap is 1.2 points after it. A fencer typing in a published score would find that confusing.
  3. The simulator only. The per-place calculator is lost, and it is the quickest answer to "how much for 3rd place of 8?".
Q2 · Order of the sections
  1. Rules first, then the tools, as in §2. recommended It is a regulation annex. The "In short" box at the top links straight to the calculator.
  2. Tools first, rules below, as in the 29 September proposal. This is quicker for someone who only wants a number, but it puts the rules below a 64 × 64 grid.
Q3 · How is the page reviewed before the engine exists?
  1. A local review copy in doc/plans/. recommended Nothing is published, and ADR-085 is not touched.
  2. A published, time-limited board preview, as on 28 September. That needs a new ADR-085 §2 amendment, its own tests, and its deletion at the release.
