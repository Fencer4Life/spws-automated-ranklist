What this plan builds

  • Admission (international events only): identity match (MATCH.ID) and a PPW or MPW result in any season → stored, whatever the printed country; otherwise printed POL → PENDING; otherwise rejected, never PENDING, no fencer created. Past seasons stay ungated.
  • One module, python/pipeline/international_admission.py, called by s6_resolve_identity, ResolveFencers, resolve_tournament_results and the EVF sync.
  • Nationality: tbl_fencer_nationality, one federation per fencer and season, fixed by the season's earliest stored result whose source printed one; tbl_result.txt_entered_for holds that printed federation as evidence. A later start that season printing another federation does not change it; it is listed as a conflict. Nationality is read by neither the admission, the scoring nor the ranking.

Scenario walk-throughs

Six cases, surface by surface

  1. Budapest 2025, V1 men's épée, a page headed Club. Every row has no printed country. BOBUSIA (16 SPWS results), KULKA (5), KORONA Radosław, ALCSER and STOLARIK match by identity and have SPWS starts: stored, with txt_entered_for NULL. Nobody else in the bracket is ours: rejected. No season nationality is set from this event, because it printed none.
  2. KULKA's 2025/26 nationality. His earliest 2025/26 start that prints a federation prints IRL: his 2025/26 row is IRL. Every later 2025/26 start prints IRL too; no conflict. In 2026/27 he may enter for Poland, and the 2026/27 row follows his first start of that season.
  3. CISZEWSKA Barbara, PEW6efs-2025-2026, V2 women's épée, 1st, printed POL. She is on the roster but has no SPWS start: PENDING. Under P A the event cannot be committed until you link her (an identity entry) or dismiss the row (match_method: EXCLUDED).
  4. LEAHEY John, Criterium 2025, V2 men's épée, 9th, printed GBR. Our birth year 1976 puts him in V1 for 2024/25, so there is no identity match, and GBR is not POL: rejected. Once his birth year is corrected, a re-stage stores it.
  5. The daily EVF sync on CERT picks up a new EVF event. Each EVF row goes through the same module: a roster fencer with an SPWS start is stored with the federation EVF printed, a Pole without a match is PENDING in the sync's report, the rest are rejected. The sync's own fuzzy-85 match is gone.
  6. An event is rolled back or replaced (fn_replace_event_from_draft). Its results are deleted, and the trigger recomputes each affected fencer's season row from the results that remain (or removes it if none remain and it was not entered by an admin). A domestic PPW event never touches the table: domestic pages print no federation.
