Ingestion · every engine · Findings — no fixes

Organiser mistakes reach the ranklist unchecked

PEW Madrid's men's épée V2 is marked sabre in Engarde. Our pipeline would store KORONA Przemysław's épée result as sabre, or refuse the whole event. Behind that one case: the pipeline's own label check never compares weapon, gender or category, and international admission ignores gender and weapon. This page lists only what is proven, with the proof. No fix is proposed here.

02 · Errors, each proven

Nine errors

1 Madrid's source marks men's épée V2 as sabre

Engarde's competition list (getCompeForDisplay, read 31 Oct 09:28 CET) has compe="epeemale2", title "EPEE MALE 2", arme="s", sexe="m". It is épée:

  • Engarde numbers competitions in order: 5912E1 EPEE MALE 1 (arme="e"), 5912E3 EPEE MALE 2, 5912E4 EPEE MALE 3-4 (arme="e");
  • it starts at 09:00 with the épée pools; every sabre competition starts at 15:00 or later;
  • its own page heading says "EPEE MALE 2".

The same comparison of title against arme on 7 earlier Engarde tournaments (Madrid 2024 and 2025, Criterium 2025 and 2026, Stockholm 2026, Faches 2026, Budapest 2025) finds no disagreement. This one is new.

2 It hits one of our fencers

Engarde's entry list for EPEE MALE 2 includes KORONA Przemyslaw, POL. In our data he is #139: born 1976 (confirmed), men's, an SPWS starter, with 51 épée, 39 foil and 1 sabre result. For season 2026/27 his birth year gives V2, so identity admits him to this bracket.

3 Our Engarde reader would store it as sabre, then refuse the event

parse_competition_list takes the weapon from arme (python/scrapers/engarde.py, ENGARDE_WEAPON_MAP.get(comp.get("arme"))). Run on a scratch copy of Madrid's real list, with the brackets marked completed:

1. EPEE MALE 2 finished            -> ('EPEE MALE 2', 'SABRE', 'M', 'V2')
2. + TABLE SABRE MALE 2 finished   -> ('TABLE SABRE MALE 2', 'SABRE', 'M', 'V2'), ('EPEE MALE 2', 'SABRE', 'M', 'V2')
   event REFUSED: Event 'PEW2efs-2026-2027' has two sources for one bracket SABRE/M/V2:
   …/tablesabremale2 and …/epeemale2. … Nothing was written.

So an ingest between the two finals stores KORONA's épée result as a men's sabre V2 result. Any ingest after TABLE SABRE MALE 2 finishes (it starts at 17:00) writes nothing for all of Madrid.

4 The URL→data check (Stage 7, ADR-052) cannot check weapon, gender or category

It runs inside ValidateCounts in all three ingestion flows (python/pipeline/engine/rulebook.py: domestic, PZSz, international). It compares the parse with ctx.event, the tbl_event row (stages.py:939). It reads enum_weapon, enum_gender, enum_age_category and txt_city from that row (url_validation.py:292, 324, 341). tbl_event has none of those columns: it has arr_weapons, txt_country and txt_location. Each check reads None and returns. Run on Madrid's real event row:

('SABRE','M','V2')  halts: []  warns: []
('EPEE','F','V4')   halts: []  warns: []
('FOIL','M','V9')  halts: []  warns: []        ← a category that does not exist
date one year off  -> halts: ['Date 2025-10-31 outside ±1 day of event window']

Only the date (and country) are ever checked.

5 When Stage 7 does object, the data is written anyway

A Stage 7 halt becomes the fault URL_DATA_MISMATCH; a count difference of more than one becomes COUNT_MISMATCH. Both resolve to accept_parsed (python/pipeline/plugins/remediation.py:77–81, 105–106), which says: "the flow commits the parsed result as-is; the loud part is the ALWAYS escalation." The bracket is written; a message is sent.

6 International admission ignores the bracket's gender and weapon

admit(name, country, category, season_end_year, roster, spws_starters, tournament_type) (international_admission.py:153) has no gender or weapon parameter. Identity is surname, first given name, and a birth year fitting the category. The roster it receives is every fencer, men and women (stages.py:122, db_connector.fetch_fencer_db). The rule "a man never in a women's bracket" (ADR-064) is applied only in the domestic matcher. That is why error 3 stores KORONA without complaint, and why a women's bracket labelled men's would admit our women.

7 An Engarde event can be completed with a final still running

The lifecycle counts a listing as not final only when its skip reason equals FTL's NOT_FINAL (python/pipeline/promotion/lifecycle.py:24, 65). Engarde skips a running final as not completed (<state>) (engarde.py:171). Given one committed bracket and one Engarde final still running, the day after the end date:

Engarde final still running, day after the end date -> COMPLETED []

8 A title with three or four categories is read as two

parse_engarde_category reads "FOIL MALE 1-2-3-4" as V1, V2 and "SABRE FEMALE 1-2-3" as V1, V2 (Madrid's real list). Both are skipped as combined either way; only the skip reason in the staging report is wrong.

9 PROD's PPS1e ingest sources are stale (left by my close on 26 Oct)

The reconcile log doc/staging/reconcile/SPWS-2026-2027.20261030-131647Z.md lists json_ingest_sources as different: CERT says both listings were committed; PROD still says women's épée was "dropped, not final".

03 · How error 1 was found

By eye, not by any check

I printed Madrid's competition list one row per competition (slug, title, arme, sexe, state, date) to see how the reader would classify it. The arme column read s on the EPEE MALE 2 row. I then confirmed it with Engarde's numbering, the start time, the page heading and the comparison over 7 other tournaments (error 1). Nothing in the pipeline would have flagged it; errors 4 and 6 are why.

Event-level and later checks that exist, and what they miss

  • Event identity (source_identity.py: date, name, weapons; REPAIR.URL.01–03) runs only in tools/set_event_source_urls.py and tools/phase5_runner.py, never during an ingest.
  • Two sources for one bracket and a bracket without one category refuse the event (international_sources.py); a bracket whose weapon is not the event's is skipped (INTL.WPN.01). In Madrid these see only the labels the reader chose, which is how error 3 ends in a refusal.
  • EVF parity sweep (evf_parity_sweep.py, daily, read-only) compares PROD's results with EVF's rows of the same weapon, gender, category, place and surname. It would show a mislabel only after the result is on PROD, once EVF publishes, as a Telegram message.

05 · Checked, not errors

What I looked at and found consistent

  • No man is stored in a women's bracket, any season. Budapest's three men in V2 women's épée (ATANASSOW, KORONA Przemysław, WIERZBICKI) were repaired on 1 Oct.
  • 5 women in men's domestic tournaments (GP3 and GP6 2023/24, PPW5 2024/25, two in PPW5 2025/26). They fit ADR-034 and the ADR-063 amendment: a bracket without a gender word is stored as men's, and a woman's points move to the women's list or are dropped. Not checked one by one.
  • 5 international results outside a naive birth-year category (LEAHEY IMEW 2023/24; KROCHMALSKI, KORONA ×2 IMSW 2025/26; TECŁAW MSW 2026/27). All at championships. Admission accepts the season's end year or the year before (international_admission._fits), so these fit.
  • 16 results in a weapon the fencer fenced only once (fencers with 5 or more results; MPW, championships). They look like real extra-weapon starts. Not proven either way.
  • The Engarde reader parses live pages correctly. N matched CERT on 16 of 16 stored brackets (Madrid 2025: 9 of 9; Criterium 2026: 7 of 7).
  • Our own data can speak to a bracket's label: 371 of 373 fencers have a gender; 273 of 328 fencers with results have fenced only one weapon.

06 · My earlier statements, corrected

Four things I said that were wrong

  • "5 results on PROD sit in a tournament whose gender isn't the fencer's; nothing ever flagged them." They fit ADR-034 and ADR-063 (§05). I had not read those ADRs.
  • "Stage 7 lets the sabre label through because Madrid's weapons include sabre." It never compares the weapon at all (error 4).
  • This morning's readiness sheet proposed fixing the Engarde reader alone. It is on hold: the errors are not specific to Engarde (§04).
  • "d'Artagnan offers nothing independent." It declares weapon and gender on a line of their own ("männlich, Degen") under the typed title (§04, corrected row).
