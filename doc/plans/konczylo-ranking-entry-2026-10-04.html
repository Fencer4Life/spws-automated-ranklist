02 · The rule

Entry through PPW or MPW, inside the ranking’s window

  1. The season’s rules name the entry types. On PROD, the 2026/27 Season Scoring Rules carry entry_types: ["PPW", "MPW"]. The season publishes the full ranking, with RANKING as the default mode.
  2. The ranking function keeps only admitted fencers. fn_ranking_full passes the call to its body for the season’s carry-over engine. Both bodies run the same gate (live definition on PROD, from migration 20261003000018): 
    WHERE NOT v_entry_gate
   OR EXISTS ( … WHERE er.id_fencer = el.fid
                   AND et.enum_type::TEXT = ANY (v_entry_types)
                   AND (ee.id_season = v_season_id
                        OR (p_rolling AND ee.id_season = v_prev_season_id)))
    A fencer stays only with a result of an entry type in the ranked season, or, when the ranking is rolling, in the previous season. The weapon is not checked, so a start in any weapon admits him to every weapon’s ranking.
  3. For 2026/27 the window is 2026/27 plus 2025/26. His PPW and MPW results all sit in 2024/25, outside it, and the window holds only his EVF results. The gate drops him from every weapon’s ranking.
  4. Once admitted, everything counts. The gate decides only whether he appears. After his first PPW or MPW start, the buckets count as usual: SPWS from PPW and MPW, and EVF+ from his best EVF results, including 2025/26 results carried over under the carry-over rule.

The sources are ADR-103 §6 and FR-141 (ranking entered through PPW/MPW; the rolling window decides entry, decision Q9b = A), and the handbook’s Competition and ranking model, “Ranking entry”: A fencer with only EVF, FIE or PZSz results is absent from the 2026/2027 full ranking until their first PPW or MPW start.

Worth knowing: intake and ranking ask different questions

ADR-106 stores an international result for any fencer with a PPW or MPW start in any season, which is why his 2025/26 PEW results were kept. The ranking gate looks only at the ranking’s window, the ranked season and the one before. Both were signed off: the first decides who is an SPWS fencer, the second who is in this season’s ranking.

05 · Only if you want a different rule

The rule is locked for 2026/27

The ranking rules, entry_types included, are a governed field. They locked at the season’s first score (ADR-097), and Admin cannot unlock them. Only the privileged, audited whole-season revision can change them, and it needs a board decision reference.
