03 · The rule

Exactly what happens

  1. One reader of weapon words for all three engines. Move source_identity._WEAPON_WORDS (accent folding, whole words) into one shared place and use it everywhere: épée = epee, szpada, Degen, spada, espada, varja; foil = foil, fleuret, floret, Florett, fioretto, florete; sabre = sabre, saber, szabla, sabel, Säbel, sciabola, sable. Add the missing Polish forms (szpadzie, szpadą, florecie, floretem, szabli, szablą) and Hungarian (párbajtőr, tőr, kard).
  2. The title names exactly one weapon → that is the bracket's weapon. If the declared weapon differs, it is overridden.
  3. The title names no weapon, or two → the title cannot decide; the declared weapon is used, as today.
  4. Every override is reported in the staging report and the Telegram summary: "EPEE MALE 2: weapon from the title (épée); Engarde declared sabre". It is also recorded in the run record, so promote's plan on PROD reads the same pages and makes the same choice.
  5. The override happens in the reader, before the event-level checks. At Madrid, EPEE MALE 2 becomes EPEE M V2, so "two sources for SABRE/M/V2" never arises and TABLE SABRE MALE 2 is written as sabre.
