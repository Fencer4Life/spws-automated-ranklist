Plan · ranking rule · 18 October 2026

Equal totals share a place

Fencers with exactly the same total share the place, and the next place skips: two fencers on equal points are both 16th, there is no 17th, and the next fencer is 18th. Today the database numbers them one after another in an order nothing fixes. The rule is yours (18 October, in chat). This plan builds it, records it as ADR-115, and leaves four small details for you.

1 · What you decided · 18 October, in chat

A shared place; the next place skips

“They share the same place, meaning the place below is one position further down. So if we have two 16th places due to exact same points, there is no 17th place, the next fencer stays in place 18th.”

This settles ADR-114’s open item 1, which the plan for ADR-114 sent to its own session (S2 A).

Also decided on 18 October: Q1–Q4 all A, S1 A, and the seed files follow “the ranking’s row order”.

In the FTL seed files, fencers who share a place keep the order the ranking lists them in (Q1: alphabetical), in both generators. Registration time orders only the unranked tail.

3 · The rule

Standard competition ranking

  1. A fencer’s place is one more than the number of fencers with a higher total: RANK() OVER (ORDER BY total DESC).
  2. Fencers with exactly the same total share the place; the next place is further down by the number of fencers sharing it.
  3. Nobody moves down. Every place after a tie keeps the number it has today; only the second (and third) tied fencer moves up to share the place.
  4. Totals, who is ranked, carry-over and the ADR-114 display stay as they are. ADR-114 keeps exact ties as whole numbers, and they now share the same place too.

Corrected on 18 October, during the build

This plan said the Python and TypeScript generators needed no change. The TypeScript generator needs none: it reads the rank and the order from fn_ftl_export_entries. The Python generator did need one. It took the tier from the fencer’s position in the ranking list (index + 1 in assemble_mixall_subrankings), not from the rank. Under ROW_NUMBER the two are the same; under a shared place the tied pair would land in tiers 16 and 17. It now carries fn_ranking_ppw’s rank (fetch_weapon_rankings returns it, assemble_mixall_subrankings takes it as ranks), and RANK.TIE.07 checks both generators on the same six-fencer case. That case failed first, as predicted. No organiser received such a file: the Python path feeds only the e-mail delivery, which has never run (ADR-080, 2026-09-25).

ADR-115: Equal Totals Share a Place; the Next Place Skips

Status: Accepted for the rule (decided by the user in chat on 2026-10-18); Q1–Q4 of the plan pending
Date: 2026-10-18
Amends: ADR-098 (the rank), ADR-114 (open item 1), ADR-080 (the order of fencers sharing a seed tier)
Relates to: ADR-101 (the Kadra parity oracle), ADR-083 (grants)
Source: supabase/migrations/20261018000001_equal_totals_share_place.sql, doc/plans/equal-totals-shared-place-2026-10-18.html

Context

Every ranking function numbered its rows with ROW_NUMBER() OVER (ORDER BY total DESC), so exactly equal totals took consecutive places in an order nothing fixed. On PROD on 18 October 2026 that touched 19 ties across the four seasons the site shows, 20 fencers in all. The seed export already assumed that equal scores share a rank. No rule fixed the order of equal season totals; the 2026/27 rules draft shares ex aequo places in a bracket the same way.

Decision

1 · A shared place. A fencer’s place is one more than the number of fencers with a higher total (RANK()): equal totals share the place, and the next place skips by the number sharing it (16, 16, 18).

2 · Everywhere the rank comes from. Every ranking function and variant: full, PPW and Kadra, event-FK and event-code, every branch. Everything that prints or uses the rank (list, drilldown, ODS, seed export, positions tool) follows without change.

3 · The order inside a shared place is fixed by Q1, so a list never reshuffles between two loads.

4 · Scope and wording as settled by Q2 and Q3.

5 · Seed files follow the ranking. In the FTL seed files, fencers who share a place keep the ranking’s own row order, in both generators; registration time orders only the unranked tail.

Alternatives considered

  1. Keep consecutive places, with a stable second key (e.g. the name). Rejected: the place itself would be decided by the alphabet.
  2. Dense places (16, 16, 17). Rejected by the user: there is no 17th.
  3. A sporting tie-break (best single result, more counted results). Rejected: the regulations have no such rule, and a shared place states the truth: the totals are equal.

Consequences

  • One migration, which also aligns the organiser download’s order of a shared place; tests RANK.TIE.01–08; no change to the list, the drilldown, the ODS or the TypeScript generator; the Python generator carries the rank (§5, corrected).
  • Nobody moves down; on 18 October, 20 fencers move up to share a place.
  • A list can show a gap after a tie (16, 16, 18), as the rules modal explains (Q3).
