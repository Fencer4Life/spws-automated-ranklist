IMSW-2025-2026 left “in progress”

The problem. The world championship of November 2025 was still IN_PROGRESS, a year later, on CERT and on PROD. The daily close reads only the active season, so it never looked at it.

What it affected:

  • The 2025/26 calendar labelled it “in progress”.
  • The daily close sent a warning about it every morning.
  • The weekly re-read kept reading it on FTL: 27 requests a week, for data that no longer changes.

What it did not affect: any points. The ranking counts IN_PROGRESS and COMPLETED events the same way (vw_eligible_event). Its results also stopped carrying into 2026/27 once the next edition, MSW-2026-2027 (Tbilisi), was completed.

Done 4 Nov 2026. I set the status to COMPLETED on CERT and then PROD, with the same scoped write the daily close uses. No result was touched, and vw_calendar shows it completed on both.

Where I went wrong. I made this wait for G70, reading the event’s dates at FTL, and so for a working FTL login. The status write reads nothing from FTL. G70’s stated reason does not hold for a past event either: “a wrong end date misleads the daily close and the sync window”. The close reads only the active season, the sync window ended a year ago, and the carry-over ended with Tbilisi. G70 has nothing left to protect.

A side effect: no open event is on FTL any more, so Monday’s and Friday’s re-reads read nothing from FTL. The two scheduled checks (9 and 13 Nov) therefore have nothing to check. The proof that the stored session is reused waits for the next FTL read.

G65 · two-day brackets

A bracket fenced over two days is never “final”

The problem. At the European championship in Plovdiv (IMEW-2024-2025), FTL lists three men’s épée brackets twice, as “(Day 1)” and “(Day 2)”, behind one link. Our reader calls such a bracket final only when both rows say Finished. A year and a half later they still don’t. My guess is that the Day 1 row never says Finished; it has not been read.

What it affects today: nothing. PROD holds all three brackets; they were stored before this rule existed.

When it would bite. Only during a championship that FTL lists this way:

  • While the event is open, a two-day bracket never counts as final, so the event stays open.
  • The daily close then completes the event without that bracket.
  • Those are the largest brackets, worth 1.3 times the points at a European championship, and they stay out of the ranking until someone ingests them by hand.

The next championship is IMEW-2026-2027, on 5–9 May 2027. I have not checked whether Tbilisi 2026 had two-day brackets.

Cost: one FTL read of Plovdiv’s schedule (a few requests), then a test-first change. Any time before May 2027 will do.
