Reguły rankingu na sezon — a pill at the bottom of the screen

A pill pinned to the bottom of the visible screen names the season chosen in the dropdown. It opens a modal drawn from that season's own definition: its sum, its buckets with one ★ per counted result, who is ranked, and its coefficients. What the fencer sees

The pill, and the modal it opens

  • The pill. It reads „ⓘ Reguły rankingu na sezon 2026/2027” and floats in the middle of the bottom of the visible screen. It stays there while the list scrolls under it.
    • Computer: the light-blue pill style approved in the mock, with a soft shadow so it reads above the rows.
    • Phone: the same pill at a 44 px touch height.
  • The strip is unchanged. „kat. 1 — roczniki: …” is back exactly as on main: same markup, same CSS. So are the title, logo, filters, switch, ODS button and table.
  • It follows the dropdown. It shows only once the rules read for the chosen season have arrived, so it never names one season while holding another's rules.
    • A season with no stored rules (2023/24) shows no pill.
    • The pill appears only on the ranklist, never on the calendar or admin views.
  • The modal is unchanged from revision 1.
    • Header: the title „Reguły rankingu na sezon 2026/2027”, then the season code, its state and „w każdej broni, płci i kategorii”.
    • The sum: „SPWS + EVF+ = Razem”, or one „Punkty” box for a PPW-only season.
    • One card per pool: each bucket shows its types, its rule and one ★ per counted result. MPW gets a single blue ★; PPW has 2 in 2026/27 and 4 in 2025/26.
    • Facts: „Kto jest w rankingu” (only when the season stores entry_types), „Ranking kroczący” (active season only), „Współczynniki”.
    • Footer: the annex link (D1) and the ★ note.
    • Closing: ✕, Esc and a click outside close it, and focus returns to the pill. On a phone the modal is a full-screen sheet with a sticky title.
    • Polish and English, with Polish plurals.

Verified · the bottom of the screen

How the pill stays on the bottom edge

ConcernHow it is handled
Finding the bottomposition: fixed; bottom: calc(16px + env(safe-area-inset-bottom)), centred with left: 50%; transform: translateX(-50%). The browser pins it to the visible screen; nothing is measured in JavaScript.
iPhoneThe safe-area inset keeps it above the home bar. Safari's toolbar showing or hiding moves the visible bottom, and the pill moves with it.
Nothing hidden behind itWhile the pill shows, a 72 px spacer (aria-hidden) ends the ranklist view. The last row, and the CT/PD switch on CERT, scroll clear of it. No existing container changes its CSS.
Drawer, modals, error bannerz-index: 50 keeps it above the list but under the drawer (99/100, Sidebar.svelte), every modal (1000) and the error banner (9999). They cover it.
WordPressChecked today on the live page weteraniszermierki.pl/znajdz-zawody, which uses the theme a ranklist page would use. None of the boxes around the embedded app has a transform, filter, perspective, contain, will-change or backdrop-filter, so fixed pins to the real screen there. The shadow root does not change this.
Narrow screensmax-width: calc(100vw - 32px). On a 375 px phone the Polish label measures about 290 px and fits on one line.
KeyboardThe pill follows the table in the DOM, so Tab reaches it after the rows. After Esc, focus returns to it.

Walk-throughs

Scenarios, surface by surface

  1. A fencer opens the ranklist on a phone. 2026/27 loads and the pill appears on the bottom edge. They scroll a long V2 épée list and the rows slide under the pill. At the end, the last row stops above it.
  2. They tap the pill. The full-screen sheet opens over everything, pill included. They close it and are back where they were, with focus on the pill.
  3. They switch to 2025/26. The pill hides while the list reloads, then reads „…2025/2026”. The modal shows „Punkty”, PPW ★★★★ and MPW ★.
  4. They switch to 2023/24. There are no stored rules, so there is no pill and no spacer. The list ends where it always did.
  5. They switch seasons quickly. The existing generation counter drops stale results. The pill is tied to the season id its rules were read for, so it never names the wrong season.
  6. They open the menu. The drawer and its shade cover the pill.
  7. They switch to the calendar. The pill disappears with the ranklist view.
  8. WordPress. The same behaviour inside the shadow root (theme checked above). The annex link depends on D1.
