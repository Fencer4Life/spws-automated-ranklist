Two things I changed that you should know about

A parity defect in the new SQL, fixed

The strategy computed ∛K as POWER(K, 1.0/3). In Postgres, 1.0/3 stops at twenty digits, so ∛8 came out as 1.999…. On a tie at the final rounding the database went down where your signed-off table goes up. The example is N = 8, 3rd of 8, at a 0.75 coefficient: 19.87 instead of 19.88. The strategy now uses the exact cube root, and a new test (SE27.ENG.14) pins it. No stored score was affected, because the engine has not scored anything yet. With today's coefficients (1, 1.2, 2) the case cannot arise.

An RTM row I had broken earlier, repaired

During the database step, an edit meant for the SE27.ING test row landed in the FR-139 requirement row. FR-139 then had an extra column, and SE27.ING still read "Planned". Both rows are correct again.

Your change of 28 September: PPS and MPS on EVF classic

  • Built on LOCAL: the migration assigns 2026/2027 PPW and MPW to the SPWS engine and the six other types to EVF classic. SE27.TYPE.02, TYPE.04 and CALC.01 pinned it first (RED, then GREEN), and SE27.ING.05 now proves a PZSz result keeps K, m and b under either engine.
  • A PZSz senior field is scored as it was before ADR-103: the full field as N, the original place and EVF classic. The coefficients are PROD’s own, PPS × 1.1 and MPS × 1.3, and the migration does not touch them.
  • ADR-103 carries a dated amendment, and ADR-100’s note, FR-137, spec §8.1.4 and Appendix C, and the handbook are corrected. On the annex, PPS and MPS show under EVF, and its coefficient slider offers PPW and MPW only.
  • Observed while checking (read-only): PROD holds the coefficients set on 21 September (MEW 1.3, MSW 1.4, PSW 1.1, PPS 1.1, MPS 1.3), while CERT still holds the older defaults (MEW and PSW 2.0, MSW 1.2, PPS and MPS 1.0). Both are unlocked with nothing scored. If PPW1 is ingested on CERT first, CERT scores with CERT’s values; this matters only for the types that differ, not for PPW or MPW.
