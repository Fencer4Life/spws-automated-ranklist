FR-176 · change after release · 22 Nov 2026

Money without decimal places

Your word: remove the two decimal places; fees are whole amounts. Nothing is changed yet. The decimals were there because the signed plan fixed „always 2 decimals” for every money figure. One case needs your decision first.

main 8f9ee544 · FR-176 released SIGNED by the user in chat 2026-11-22 („Sign off A, release”)

01 · What changes

Whole amounts everywhere

  • The page (screen and print): „5750 EUR”, „50 EUR”, „11 910 EUR” instead of „5750,00 EUR”.
  • The .ods, both tabs: the money format loses its fixed decimals. Checked in LibreOffice before writing this page: „70 EUR”, „1 480 EUR”, no trailing comma.
  • Fees, tier totals, the entries' fees and every grand total are sums of whole fees, so they are always whole.

02 · One question

Q1 · A share that is not a whole amount

The table „Według broni i płci” splits the fee of a multi-weapon entry equally between its weapons (R3). On PROD today Łomianki charges 50 / 75 / 100 EUR: a two-weapon entry puts 37,50 into each of its two weapons, a three-weapon entry 33,33 into each of three. Only this table's cells can be fractional; its grand total is whole.

Recommendation: A

A — whole amounts show no decimals; a share that is not whole shows its fraction („37,5 EUR”, „33,33 EUR”). What you see always adds up to the total you see. One format does both, on the page and in the file.

B — no decimals anywhere: shares are rounded for display („38 EUR”). The values stay exact underneath, so totals are right, but a column can look off by one or two: 38 + 38 shown above a total of 75.
