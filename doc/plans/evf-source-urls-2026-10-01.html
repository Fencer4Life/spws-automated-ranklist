Why the data is wrong

Root cause, in one paragraph

The Phase 5 draft writer (python/pipeline/review_cli.py:899, commit fc5ac389, 2 May 2026) sets each tournament's N to the number of matched fencers in its V-category group. For a domestic event that is the whole field, because everyone is ingested. For an EVF event, ADR-038 drops every non-Polish row before matching, so N became the Polish head-count, and the places were renumbered among the Poles. The EVF score depends on both, so those scores are wrong. They feed the 2026/27 ranking through carry-over until each event's 2026/27 edition is scored. The fix plan for every ingestion path and for the stored data follows this sheet.
