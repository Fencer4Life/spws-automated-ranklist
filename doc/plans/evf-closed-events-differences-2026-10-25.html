§ 3 · a gap in the daily check (P5)

It would report two kinds of false difference

The parity check released on 25 Oct (python/pipeline/evf_parity_sweep.py, pair_evf_event) pairs an event only by its start date, within three days of EVF’s date. This run shows where that fails:

  • A championship longer than three days. EVF dated Plovdiv by its last day, so the check would have reported “no EVF event” every day for a result set that matches exactly. The next one is the next European Championships.
  • Two EVF events on one day (an épée and a sabre event, 6 Apr 2024). The check reports “ambiguous” when the weapons alone would pair them.
  • A fencer EVF lists without a place. EVF gives him place 9999 and counts him in N (Dublin, Criterium 2026). The check reports a difference of ours, when it is EVF counting an unplaced fencer, which your rule tells us not to copy.

The check writes nothing, so none of this harms data. It is noise in the daily Telegram line, and the noise would teach the reader to ignore it.
