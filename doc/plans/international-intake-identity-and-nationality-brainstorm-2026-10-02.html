Who an international result belongs to, and which country he fenced for

Two questions that used to be one. Who is this? is answered by identity, against our roster, for every ingestion path through one shared module. Which country did he fence for? is a fact per season, and a fencer can have more than one. The intake rule is decided; the nationality model is what we are designing now.

Decided 2 October · N = A, with the season lock

How do we model several nationalities per fencer, one chosen per season?

✓ Your decision: A, and a fencer cannot change nationality within a season

The first start recorded with a nationality fixes it until the end of that season; he can change it the next season. So a season holds exactly one nationality per fencer, and different seasons may differ. The bool_chosen flag below is no longer needed: the key is (id_fencer, id_season). A later start in the same season that prints another country does not change it; it is listed as a conflict for review. Deterministic under re-ingest: the earliest-dated start of the season fixes it. Recorded in ADR-106.

Your requirement: the same fencer may represent different countries in different seasons; we keep every nationality we know of, and mark the one he represents in a given season. KULKA Dawid fences PPW for a Polish club and entered EVF Budapest, Madrid and Liège 2025/26 for Ireland; LEAHEY John is GBR at every EVF event and stored as PL here.

A A season table: one row per fencer, season and country

tbl_fencer_nationality
  id_fencer     → tbl_fencer
  id_season     → tbl_season
  txt_country   three-letter federation code (POL, IRL, GBR …)
  bool_chosen   the one he represents in this season
  enum_source   SOURCE (a result printed it) | ADMIN (entered by hand)
  primary key (id_fencer, id_season, txt_country)
  at most one chosen row per (id_fencer, id_season)
  • Filled by ingestion. When a source prints a federation for a stored result, that country is added for the fencer in that season. The first one of a season becomes the chosen one. A second, different country in the same season is added but not chosen, and is listed for you to review.
  • Edited by you in the Admin fencer view: add a country, or change the chosen one.
  • KULKA 2025/26: IRL, chosen. LEAHEY: GBR, chosen, in each season with an international result.

B Validity periods: a nationality holds from a season until it changes

(id_fencer, txt_country, season_from, season_to, bool_chosen). Fewer rows, since a federation change is rare. But one chosen per season becomes a constraint over overlapping ranges, and correcting one season in the middle means splitting a period in two.

C Evidence only: the printed federation on each result, the season derived

Each international result keeps the country its source printed (tbl_result.txt_entered_for); a view lists a fencer's countries per season, and the chosen one is the most frequent, unless you override it. Every claim has a source row. But a season with only domestic results has nothing (domestic pages print no country), and “chosen” is a rule nobody sees.

Recommendation: A, with C's column as its evidence. A is your model as you stated it: several countries per season, one chosen, editable. The result-level column records what each source printed, so every row in A can be traced to the results that put it there. And timing matters: the 18 international events are about to be re-staged under the new intake rule. If the column exists by then, that one pass also fills 2024/25 and 2025/26. Without it, a second pass would be needed.

What stays as it is: tbl_fencer.txt_nationality (one value, default PL, read only by the fencer-creation dedup) stays untouched for now and is retired once A is in use. Nationality is not read by the intake: who a result belongs to is decided by identity and SPWS participation alone (below).

Decided · 2 October

The intake rule, one module for every path

For an international event (PEW, MEW, MSW, PSW, the IMEW and IMSW codes included), each row of the source is decided once, in this order:

  1. Identity. Surname, first given name and a birth year that fits the bracket's category match one fencer on our roster (the existing MATCH.ID rule), and that fencer has a PPW or MPW result in any season. The row is stored and linked. The printed country does not matter. ZABŁOCKI, LEAHEY (GBR), KULKA (IRL), BOBUSIA (no country printed).
  2. Fallback. Otherwise, if the source prints POL, the row goes to review as PENDING. It is never stored automatically.
  3. Rejected. Everything else is dismissed and listed in the staging summary. It never becomes PENDING. KOLLAR Gabriel (SVK, no SPWS start).

Past seasons stay ungated: the ranking entry gate remains on 2026/27 only. Domestic events are unchanged.
